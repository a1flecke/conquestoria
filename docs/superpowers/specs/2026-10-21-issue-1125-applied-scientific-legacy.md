# Applied scientific Legacy: consolidated player journey

20 October 2026. Advisory consolidation for [issue #1125](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/issues/1125), requested by the user. This summarizes accepted product direction and separates tentative mechanics and open definitions. It is not an implementation specification, a playtest result or approval of previously proposed numbers. Published as a planning snapshot on 21 October 2026. It describes intended product behavior; it does not change gameplay.

Scope: applied scientific Legacy, illustrated by ocean recovery. Fundamental-science and exploration candidates retain their own evidence and intellectual-use requirements; this document does not impose industrial deployment on all scientific victory paths. Water and crop examples used in calibration are not new approved finales.

## The promise

Build a civilization that discovers something consequential, makes it practical, and changes the world through its use. The undertaking should remain worth pursuing if another civilization wins first: useful knowledge, capabilities, recovered places and strategic opportunities exist before and beyond the competitive result.

Distinguish three standards:

- **Useful:** the advance has value apart from winner credit.
- **Substantial:** the participant supplies meaningful actual research toward overcoming major scientific or practical limitations, or fulfills another genuinely substantial agreed role.
- **Legacy-making:** the complete undertaking achieves its exceptional capability and required world-changing application, scale, variety and current function.

These are authoring and qualification principles, not three interchangeable point meters. Scope, practical outcomes and qualitative conditions must become explicit supported rules rather than an invisible judgment of how impressive a project feels.

## The journey

These stages overlap in play. They organize the experience rather than impose a fixed quest order: useful application, research, cooperation and contribution can develop together.

### 1. Recognize a consequential opportunity

The player encounters a problem or scientific opportunity supported by the world and legitimate information. Ocean recovery concerns a defined consequential class of persistent marine plastic that existing methods cannot practically recover and safely process at meaningful scale. A cosmetic cleanup, ordinary incremental upgrade or a single laboratory announcement is insufficient.

Present what could change, what is currently known and what remains uncertain. Existing discoveries, institutions and equipment matter. Do not manufacture pollution, hidden opportunities or new ignorance just to provide the route. A particular map may not support this candidate; opportunity availability and guidance remain design work.

### 2. Choose a national or shared undertaking

The player chooses consequential research priorities, capabilities, resources, applications and partners. Routine laboratory work and ordinary authorized operations are automatic; no per-city policies or per-discovery secrecy chores.

Independent leadership can use ordinary trade, contractors and foreign services without making every supplier a co-winner. Genuine shared participation requires explicit admission and substantial responsibilities. Wholly isolated qualification is intended to be ordinarily harder through an additional useful accomplishment, not an artificial waiting period or blanket cost multiplier; actual geography and existing capabilities can change comparative difficulty. For ocean recovery, the developed candidate extension is practical remote recovery and safe processing beyond dependence on nearby specialized shore facilities; exact sufficiency remains open.

Before real shared execution begins, explain the participant's concrete responsibility, scope, bounded resource authorization, dependencies, continuing duties and why fulfillment would count. Fixed promises cannot grow merely because the world, program or partners grow. An unfinished approach can become obsolete; this risk must be disclosed. A sufficient fulfilled role is not a guarantee that every unfinished approach will remain necessary.

### 3. Discover something that changes the available play

The ocean candidate has four accepted functional requirements:

| Required capability | What it must establish |
| --- | --- |
| Selective recovery | The declared pollution can be recovered without comparable ecological harm. |
| Responsible processing | The recovered material is safely dealt with through a complete practical chain. |
| Practical reproduction | The capability can operate beyond an exceptional one-off installation. |
| Relevant operating range | Practical proof covers meaningfully different conditions required by the candidate. |

These are not four mandatory research stages. Existing knowledge and ordinary operation can satisfy requirements. Research addresses real gaps and uncertainty. Applicable evidence transfers; changing a project name or moving to comparable conditions does not create new science.

An advance should change a feasible action, warranted understanding, dependency or useful outcome. Fundamental and enabling findings can have complementary or delayed benefits; every experiment does not need a separate button or commercial reward. Necessary negative findings and validation can matter when they genuinely resolve uncertainty used by the successful effort.

Candidate effects include access to previously untreatable habitats, practical recovered industrial inputs, repeatable production and wider reliable operating reach. Specific methods and tradeoffs remain proposed content, not approved chemistry or implemented subsystems. Research unlocks capability; actual safe application produces ecological recovery.

### 4. Turn capability into substantial application

Choose real locations, operating arrangements and the important tradeoffs of capacity, access, independence and adaptation. Results must extend beyond a few tiles or repeated tiny demonstrations. Geographic reach and environmental variety are both necessary; neither substitutes for the other.

The map and ordinary civilization views show actual changes. A fishery impaired by the target pollution may function again; a habitat can recover without compulsory exploitation; a treatment chain can supply usable inputs if its real outputs support that. No universal bonus or cosmetic map recoloring substitutes for the declared result.

Routine collection, transport, treatment and monitoring follow standing direction. Material new spending, responsibilities or knowledge-sharing require a substantive decision. Basic usability and automation are not locked behind research. Proposed notices emphasize the discovery, the responsible institutions and a legitimately known opportunity; exact UI is not final.

### 5. Earn a contribution through actual useful work

Record effective relevant research that advances genuine unresolved requirements. Count useful work once. Idle capacity, raw expenditure, repeated known tests, membership and the final click cannot establish substantial authorship. Efficient equivalent fulfillment preserves its reference value.

Several civilizations can jointly develop one inseparable discovery. They need not invent private components. Six meaningful co-researchers in the accepted successful example can all be eligible; that is not a promise that any six entrants can divide any remaining work into sufficient roles. The universal 20%-of-program floor was rejected. Contribution significance is assessed independently of consortium size; the numerical benchmark remains open.

Keep three milestones distinct:

| Milestone | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Promised provision delivered | The participant supplied the agreed work or capability. A failed enterprise can still leave that responsibility fulfilled. |
| Contribution demonstrated in success | The work actually participates in the successful capability/application. Unused effort is not authorship of an unrelated success. |
| Shared victory qualified | The collective result and the participant's actual admission, contribution, survival and applicable obligation conditions all hold. |

After a contribution is earned, later damage can prevent the collective finish without erasing that history. Restoration does not require re-earning past accomplishments. Expulsion, new sponsorship or renaming cannot confiscate protected contributions. Explicit continuing duties, withdrawal and qualifying breach rules remain relevant.

### 6. Adapt to competition and setbacks

Independent discoveries, theft and sabotage remain possible. Obtaining advanced knowledge or equipment does not automatically supply the capability to understand or reproduce it. Existing knowledge-spread and intelligence rules continue; no micromanagement of every discovery is added here.

An independent advance can supersede unfinished research. Retain findings actually used and offer genuine remaining responsibilities where available, with informed acceptance of changed obligations. No guaranteed replacement, manufactured research or automatic compensation victory.

Partners may legitimately out-research each other, even deliberately. They do not own exclusive rights to unsolved problems. Denying expressly promised access or attacking a partner's work is separately assessed as a potentially disqualifying serious attributable breach. Concealed betrayal does not automatically trigger an omniscient eligibility penalty. Physical effects, established responsibility, historical contribution and shared entitlement remain distinct; reconciliation remains possible under its own rules.

Damage matters through actual lost functions. Valid substitutes can restore those functions. A recovered self-sustaining ecosystem does not need an obsolete cleanup installation kept alive solely for credit; separately required practical capabilities must still exist.

### 7. Culminate through the normal game

An automatic limited contender warning precedes the decisive final work, including an anonymous notice for unmet civilizations. It must permit meaningful response without revealing secret locations, methods or private contribution state. Exact triggers and lead time remain open.

At the first qualifying common checkpoint, the game assesses the actual world-changing outcome and eligible surviving participants. Shared winners receive an equal win; ordinary suppliers and unfinished members do not automatically join them. There is no extra ceremony, approval vote, final research chore or generic holding period after all requirements are met.

The ending should show what changed and remember the institutions, places and contributions that caused it. Historical credit and winner status can differ. The competitive result stays settled if the player continues afterward.

## Tentative mechanics and unapproved values

- **Simultaneous research:** proportional attribution of comparable effective work within one common research interval, capped by genuine remaining work. Earlier work stays unchanged. This is a tentative prototype choice, not accepted balance. Occasional voluntary coordination may be acceptable; recurring last-point allocation management fails the experience test.
- **Magnitude and work weights:** 100 as a national-advance reference, the ocean 240/240/160/160 weights, effective rates and resulting participant totals are illustrative. No numerical substantial-contribution minimum is approved. Actual gaps determine actual remaining work; no compulsory 800-work bill.
- **Ocean reach:** three regional applications, 4% each and two challenge families are prior proposals, not adopted thresholds. Exact reference geography, environmental equivalence and functional recovery definitions remain open.
- **Concrete content:** particular recovery/processing methods, production models, seasonal options and presentation treatments are proposed examples. Acceptance of the four functional requirements did not approve every example.

## Work still needed before implementation

| Package | Remaining definition or evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Capability and scope | Define supported target pollution, outcomes, practical limitations, evidence, reference geography and meaningful environmental differences. |
| Contributions | Calibrate independent magnitude and work values against authored major advances; define attribution where distinct findings and simultaneous incremental work meet. |
| Time and resources | Define research resolution boundaries, capacity/absorption, consumption and any authorized reuse of unused capacity without inventing bankable work. |
| Commitments and information | Finish applicable breach/evidence details, shared visibility, role forecasts, amendments and exact contender-warning triggers. |
| Experience and balance | Test compact specialists, larger empires, six substantive collaborators, late entry, normal cooperation, supersession, setbacks, AI choices and attention burden. Validate suitable opportunity frequency across maps. |

Some entries are product definitions, not merely numbers to tune after coding. Paper examples establish possible consistency, not frequency, difficulty or enjoyment. The conceptual research-contribution branch is consolidated; the applied-science system is not implementation-ready.
