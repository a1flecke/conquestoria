## Target capability and scientific requirements

Target a consequential defined class of dispersed/weathered marine plastic that ordinary methods cannot practically recover and safely process at relevant scale. Do not claim removal of every plastic, universal pristine oceans or a measured real-world health benefit. The invented capability must materially expand what civilization can repair and be practically reproducible. Discovery alone, collection alone or more efficient ordinary cleanup cannot fulfill the Legacy.

Four functional requirements can contain research in this authored starting situation. They are not four mandatory new technologies in every campaign, separate winner seats, exclusive inventions or a fixed order. Known applicable solutions transfer. Field evidence and already prepared findings can resolve relevant work. Construction, operating capacity and permission alone do not become scientific advances.

The last row is finite meaningful missing knowledge in this specific scenario, not an endless pool of new tests for every tile or named ocean. If existing engineering already handles conditions, apply it without manufacturing scientific work. The reproduction row concerns genuinely unresolved process engineering, not awarding research for buying a second factory or filing a report. Resource use, capacity and ecological benefit remain real constraints; no new chemistry simulation or per-lab administration is authorized.

## Experience and failure criteria

Make the choices about methods and consequences: concentrate resources on safe processing to unlock earlier limited recovery; seek marine expertise to expand the feasible environments; use an existing industrial partner versus develop independent process capability; prepare substantial applications while research proceeds. Routine laboratories, observation and supported operation follow standing direction. Early safe recovery has actual effects even while other regional applications remain unready. No forced ecological extraction or global bonus substitutes for those effects.

This candidate fails if players must repeatedly lower research to preserve friends' eligibility; if all four problems are interchangeable payments; if compact expertise is routinely replaceable by generic spending; if existing solutions still trigger compulsory research; or if significant discovery gives no usable capability until the final win. The paper arithmetic cannot determine how frequently any failure occurs.

Recommendation: review the four functional requirements and these contributor profiles as the concrete candidate, retaining all numerical work values as trial settings. The largest outstanding calibration dependency is the independent national-advance benchmark; cross-candidate comparison must anchor it before implementation. ## Distinct choices and civilization payoffs — proposed 20 October

### Lasting-value audit — refinement accepted 20 October

The user's subsequent “Agreed” accepts the refinement: preserve all four functional requirements while letting existing knowledge, genuine discoveries and practical operation satisfy them naturally. Research arises only from actual knowledge or capability gaps; no mandatory four-stage technology ladder or research fee for already feasible deployment. Necessary validation and enabling findings can be useful without standalone rewards. The illustrative 800-work program is not a mandatory bill: solved requirements reduce remaining research opportunity without inventing replacement work or guaranteeing the same number of eligible researchers. Numerical calibration, exact capability definitions and playability remain unresolved. Selective recovery and responsible processing have clear candidate benefits when they actually unlock feasible safe recovery and a practical treatment chain. Practical reproduction and environmental adaptation are conditional: both can be major advances, but both can also degenerate into charging research for permission to repeat an already understood operation.

Keep all four accepted functional requirements. Do not force four separate inventions or paid research stages. Reproduction merits distinct research when the prototype depends on a genuinely unresolved process: for example, an essential component cannot yet be manufactured reproducibly at operating scale. Solving that opens repeat production and genuinely new operating capacity. If capable facilities can already reproduce the method, ordinary construction and operation provide evidence; no extra replication research bar. Environmental adaptation merits distinct research when an actual limitation prevents useful safe operation under the relevant conditions. If known equipment and an authorized operating plan already work, use them and obtain relevant proof through normal operation rather than inventing adaptation work.

Necessary validation can still resolve real uncertainty and be useful research even if it confirms an existing hypothesis rather than inventing equipment. It changes a justified operating decision, supports safe application or rules out an unsound approach. Repeated verification after sufficient applicable evidence creates no extra progress. The lasting-value test applies to meaningful advances and the integrated program, not to every experiment, research tick or supporting finding. Fundamental/enabling knowledge may have delayed or complementary payoffs; do not require a standalone commercial benefit, new button or arbitrary bonus from every investigation.

Concrete contrast: an existing method works in temperate open water but damages a sensitive estuary under its current collection settings. A genuine selective advance can enable safe intervention there if the whole treatment chain is available. The program's practical reproduction problem might be manufacturing that selective component consistently, not licensing paperwork. If cold-water operation merely needs an already known equipment configuration, configuration and field operation supply the proof; no fourth mandatory discovery. These are fictional authored conditions, not claims of real chemistry, new required failure rolls or an imposed story in every campaign.

Proposed authoring check for each major advance: identify an actual prior limitation; state the changed capability or decision justified by new knowledge; identify the real means of application; name an observable world consequence; and verify that the benefit is worthwhile without winner credit. If only a permission label or victory percentage changes, merge the stage into ordinary operation, recast it as necessary evidence of real uncertainty, or remove its separate research cost. Preserve the achievement requirement when removing a redundant research stage.

Implication for prior numbers: 240/240/160/160 describes only the constructed starting situation with all four genuine gaps. It is not a fixed mandatory ocean-research bill. Removing an already solved gap reduces remaining scientific opportunity; do not transfer its work budget to other tasks merely to preserve expected winner capacity. Trial participant arithmetic must be rerun against the actual unresolved work before offers of sufficient roles. **Selective recovery changes where intervention is feasible.** Candidate approaches are concentrated high-throughput recovery suitable to some waste distributions, and precise selective recovery able to work in sensitive habitats but requiring different equipment and lower throughput. Both must meet safety in their valid settings; knowingly damaging an ecosystem is not a qualifying alternative. A choice concerns the actual region and purpose, not a universal best tier. New capability makes appropriate known regions actionable in the existing map. Actual ecological benefits require the whole safe recovery/treatment chain and real recovery; the unlock alone cannot restore wildlife. Players may pursue both approaches through real development, with no arbitrary permanent doctrine lock.

**Responsible processing changes the use of recovered material and the location of treatment.** Candidate fictional methods can favor high-quality recovered industrial feedstock at sophisticated shore facilities, or safe treatment with lower material returns and a different practical support footprint. Both have bounded real inputs, outputs and throughput; do not imply safe means free, universally portable or automatically suitable for the isolated remote extension. Choices concern industrial benefit versus practical coverage and infrastructure dependence. Recovered inputs enter actual supported production, potentially reducing purchases of those inputs; no global economic bonus or guaranteed profit. A shortage can make valuable material recovery attractive; an urgent distant recovery may favor an accessible safe treatment arrangement. Neither routine trash transfer nor simply cheaper ordinary recycling is the exceptional scientific achievement.

**Practical reproduction changes who can build and operate the capability.** A working repeatable design enables expansion beyond the founding prototype through actually equipped domestic facilities, capable partners, or a supported service model. The scientific advance is resolving real process/reproduction limits; licensing, purchasing and constructing facilities are downstream strategic choices and do not automatically create research authorship. Domestic expansion requires capacity and investment; sharing an adequate production package can accelerate foreign applications while spreading usable knowledge and capability. A service arrangement can retain some know-how but preserves operating dependency and does not substitute for required independent reproducibility. Known designs do not create factories or transmit all secrets automatically. Shared victory remains separate from ordinary purchase or licensing.

**Environmental adaptation changes reliable operating reach.** If an existing method can safely work in the available conditions, a standing seasonal/limited operating plan may suffice, with lower realized coverage or slower recovery. A genuinely new adaptation can widen the conditions in which sustained operation is possible. The choice can concern earlier limited benefits versus investment in broader capability; suitable alternate regions can remain legitimate where the full variety/scope standard is still met. Routine pauses and resumption are automatic. No per-turn weather management, compulsory new research for every region, or guarantee seasonal operation will achieve the required result under continuing pressures. Remote independence is its own substantial extension; generic environmental adaptation does not silently award it.

The choices are contextual and can coexist. Each method requires concrete authored inputs, outputs, limitations and capabilities before implementation, but avoid creating detailed chemical recipes or unsupported economy/weather subsystems merely to justify the prose. Known infrastructure may make one option clearly preferable in a specific campaign; no need to force a dilemma every time. Preserve knowledge and prior useful work when changing application strategy; real relocation, refitting, capacity and commitment changes still have consequences.

### Presentation, progress and identity

Use the existing map/project/city views. A major discovery communicates the actual capability, the credited institutions and one legitimately known opportunity. An illustrative notice is “Kestrel's marine institute has demonstrated selective recovery. The existing Northbank operation can now treat the estuary safely with its current processing support.” Show this only if those facts hold. New practical regions and limits appear in contextual previews; ordinary usability and automation are available regardless of technology. Safe improvements within existing authority may take effect automatically; major construction, spending, knowledge-sharing or duty changes require the player's substantive choice.

Actual application changes the world: relevant habitat recovers, a fishery impaired by this pollution can function again, supported recovered material reaches industry, or a previously dependent region gains real operating capacity. Restored nature can remain unexploited. Do not create universal happiness, health, food or production rewards detached from the represented outcome. A brief regional before/after view, changed facilities/vessels and a durable institutional history can make the accomplishment recognizable without per-tile celebration spam. Other threats and sources of pollution remain governed by their actual effects.

These payoffs should remain useful if another civilization wins first or the player pursues a different Legacy. Builders can develop production and recovery infrastructure, protectors can restore sensitive regions, merchants can operate meaningful services, explorers can open difficult operating regions, and scientific collaborators can contribute expertise. These are uses of actual capabilities, not five new victory types, automatic co-winner roles or claims that every personality has been playtested. Peaceful cooperation can be satisfying without compulsory sabotage or crisis.
