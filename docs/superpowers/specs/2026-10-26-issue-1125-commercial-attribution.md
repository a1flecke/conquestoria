# Commercial Legacy: attribution without a competition over credit

Advisory design record for issue #1125, published as a planning snapshot on 26 October 2026 at the user's request. The user's “Agreed” accepts the attribution direction summarized below. No implementation or empirical playtest. Existing fixed substantial roles, independent claims, consent, continuity, survival and common-checkpoint rules remain controlling.

## Accepted attribution rule

Count the functioning economic result once. Recognize each civilization's actual substantial contribution to that result. Different complementary contributions can cover overlapping activity; splitting the same delivered contribution does not multiply it. Determine victory eligibility separately through a complete independent achievement or an explicitly agreed and fulfilled substantial shared role.

This extends the existing distinction between historical attribution, contribution-linked extent and winner eligibility. It does not turn every credited civilization into a shared winner or reduce an earned contributor's credit because another civilization also deserves recognition.

Acceptance includes bounded attribution for parallel provision and shared financing: splitting the same delivered work cannot give every participant credit for the whole provision. Paid assistance retains genuine authorship without automatically granting shared winner status. Routine attribution is automatic; substantial shared roles remain explicit and fixed under the existing rules. Ownership, operating authority, historical contribution and current functioning extent remain distinct.

The numerical examples illustrate the accepted rule; they do not calibrate economic costs, contribution thresholds or victory balance. Precise causal predicates and pooled-finance accounting still require specification against the eventual economic representation. The comparison and recommendation wording below are retained as rationale, not evidence of implementation or player testing.

## Alternatives and why this one fits

| Approach | Consequence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Award the result to the founder, owner, biggest spender or last contributor | Easy headline, but erases complementary work, rewards expensive inefficiency or last-step sniping, and makes partners resist helping | Reject |
| Divide all credit into a single 100% pool using spending, shipments or actions | Adding a useful collaborator can dilute existing credit; financial and scientific/logistical contributions become misleadingly interchangeable | Reject as the general attribution rule |
| Bound each contribution by the real work it delivered and the functioning result it helped establish; deduplicate outcome coverage | Supports specialization and honest overlap, but requires clear rules for same-work subdivisions and minor assistance | Recommend |

Do not confuse the rejected general credit pool with accounting for two parties financing the same procurement. Apportioning that one finite financial provision is appropriate. It does not apportion all authorship of the resulting economy.

## What other games establish

Guild Wars 2's developer explicitly connects shared experience/loot and shared resource access with making other players welcome collaborators. PC Gamer's archived 2012 review awarded 94 and praised cooperative roaming; its later retrospective also emphasizes cooperation instead of competition over rewards. This supports the experience goal of rewarding help without taking earned recognition away. It is not evidence that minimal participation should earn a civilization-game victory. [ArenaNet design explanation](https://www.guildwars2.com/en/news/is-it-fun-colin-johanson-on-how-arenanet-measures-success/), [PC Gamer review and retrospective](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/mmo/guild-wars-2-review-2012/).

Age of Wonders 4's June 2023 Wyvern update stopped AI accepting player surrender as a vassal when that would make the player or AI allied with all living players with allied victory enabled. This is a concrete membership-related shortcut addressed by the developers. Borrow the need to distinguish a diplomatic status change from earning a result. Do not import a waiting period or infer that all allied victories were poorly received. The source does not concern bypassing a victory timer; an earlier commentary description of it was corrected. [Official patch notes, Diplomacy](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/age-of-wonders-4/news/age-of-wonders-4-wyvern-update-patch-notes).

Victoria 3's 1.7 ownership diary separates ownership of building levels from their location and production, with mixed ownership and a registry for inspection. A developer reply explains that owner buildings were assigned zero urbanization because otherwise the same industrial activity would score urbanization again through its owner. This is a useful concrete analogy: represent multiple relationships to an economic result without duplicating the result. These are historical design notes, not claims about current balance or reception. [Diary and developer replies](https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/victoria-3-dev-diary-110-building-ownership-foreign-investment.1647879/).

The resulting design inference combines generous recognition with demanding victory eligibility and unique world accounting. None of these sources validates our commercial thresholds.

## Attribution in four ordinary questions

1. **What actually changed?** Identify the substantial productive capability or newly feasible operating extent that now functions. Use represented before/after capacity, access, supply and actual use. An industry rising from 60 to 100 does not automatically make all 100 new opportunity.
2. **What did this civilization actually deliver?** Identify its real capacity, adaptation, investment, integration, access arrangement or other substantive commercial provision. The category label cannot establish significance by itself. Ordinary trade can accumulate into substantial provision; a designated megaproject is unnecessary.
3. **Where is that contribution realized?** Link it only to the actual supported functioning activity, bounded by the delivered provision and relevant capacity. Unrelated industries, remote ancestry and merely incidental supplies do not inherit the whole result.
4. **What claim does that support?** Historical recognition alone does not establish substantial contribution, and substantial contribution alone does not establish victory. Assess a complete independent claim or the already agreed shared role using the existing rules.

These must be deterministic, understandable content rules, not a retrospective advisor judgment or an imagined simulation of what would have happened without each civilization. A contribution need not be uniquely irreplaceable, largest, most expensive or permanently indispensable. An efficient contributor should not lose credit because another spends more.

## Complementary work versus subdivisions of the same work

**Complementary case:** Auren establishes substantial reliable transport capacity supporting a new 60-unit industrial application. Brin develops and delivers the substantial industrial integration used throughout the same 60. Both links can cover 60 if their actual provisions support that scope and meet the substantial-contribution rules. The application still represents only 60 in the world and in their collective claim.

**Same-work case:** Auren and Brin instead split financing for that one 60-unit provision evenly. Financing alone links each to 30 units of supported provision, not 60 each. The aggregate financial provision is still 60. If provision is divisible, use the actual financed deliveries/capacity. For pooled indivisible provision, apportion that funding role by each participant's real funded share of the eligible delivered provision. This is a proposed bounded financial accounting rule, not a general division of historical authorship.

The same principle applies to parallel suppliers: two operators each carrying half the required throughput do not each claim to have delivered all throughput. A civilization may combine contributions: if its finance supports 30 units and its substantial integration genuinely covers all 60, its unique contribution-linked coverage can be 60, not 90.

Do not let a change of labels convert subdivisions into complementary roles. Two payments named “development finance” and “strategic finance” remain payments toward the same provision. Five nominal contractors splitting one delivery do not manufacture five substantial functions. Real amounts, capacity and fulfilled responsibilities remain the evidence.

Funding attribution concerns net real provision actually financed, not gross invoiced turnover, the most expensive invoice or money circulated among friendly treasuries. Inflating prices cannot increase the application's extent. Reimbursements after completion do not purchase authorship. Exact accounting for pooled finance, refunds and replacement funding must be specified against the eventual economic representation; do not claim a raw gold-share formula is sufficient for every case.

Substantial financing can still be the entire basis of a real shared role. It need not be charitable or the only possible source. Its complete fixed promised delivery must be fulfilled. Ordinary paid assistance does not automatically confer shared status, while a paid provider's genuine authorship is not erased by payment. A provider can independently pursue a complete qualifying commercial achievement across its real contributions; a buyer has no veto over that independent claim.

## Checked three-region example

Use an applicable whole-world reference of 1,000, already including the completed applications. These are analyst-known figures, not a proposal to reveal hidden world totals. All three applications are in independent third-party economies, so counted extent is foreign from either contender's perspective. Assume the stated substantive contributions are genuinely established; arithmetic alone cannot prove them.

| Application | Unique functioning extent | Auren's substantial contribution covers | Brin's substantial contribution covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kestrel region | 120 | 120 through the delivered commercial access/supply program | 60 through complementary integration of a defined part |
| Senna region | 80 | 80 through the same substantive program's actual provision there | 0 |
| Daru region | 120 | 0 | 120 through delivered integration there |
| Totals | **320** | **200** | **180** |

Their overlap is 60. Auren has 20% contribution-linked opportunity extent and Brin 18%; neither meets the independent 30% total trial. The unique collective result is 320/1,000 = 32%, all foreign, so the shared extent passes the 30/20 trial. It is not 38% from adding contributor totals, and the economy does not gain another 60 because two civilizations helped.

If they agreed shared participation before completion, each fulfilled its own fixed substantial role, and every other required condition holds, both can earn an equal shared win. This example assumes those roles were independently valid when activated; it does not introduce a new personal percentage threshold. Without that agreement, their separate incomplete claims do not automatically merge into a shared win. Preserve the existing prohibition on retroactively relabeling completed work as admission.

A minor supplier's final routine delivery earns payment and appropriate history, not automatic coverage of all 320 or a winner seat. This is true even if that delivery happened to finish the last missing dependency. A late entrant with a genuinely substantial unfinished role can still qualify; late timing alone is not disqualifying and no minimum membership duration is added.

## Stress cases

| Event | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Auren's same Kestrel capacity appears in three contracts | The same supported activity still counts once within Auren's claim. |
| Brin supplies a complementary substantial function in Kestrel | Recognize Brin without subtracting Auren's actual contribution. Joint extent remains 120 there. |
| Two financiers divide one 60-unit provision into equal real funding responsibilities | Each financing link covers 30; combined funded coverage is 60. No duplicate whole-program credit. |
| One financier also develops a substantial complementary capability covering the same 60 | Its unique coverage can be 60, not 30 plus 60. |
| Kestrel adds 80 activity using independently established capacity unrelated to Auren's delivered program | Auren does not automatically inherit it. Its existing 120 link remains bounded; actual continued use of Auren's provision would require separate evidence. |
| Kestrel learns to operate the established application independently | Preserve genuine opportunity contribution under existing continuity/succession rules; authority and current operating duties can change. Independence alone does not erase the achievement. |
| An authorized operator or equipment successor maintains the same achieved function | No automatic authorship reset. Do not require the original equipment, workers or supplier forever. |
| The shared 60 of Kestrel genuinely stops functioning before qualification and no continuity protection applies | Auren's active extent becomes 140, Brin's 120 and their union 260. At the unchanged reference of 1,000 the joint extent fails. Fulfilled delivery history is separate from the current functioning result. |
| An owner buys completed facilities | Ownership changes; opportunity authorship does not transfer with the deed. Actual exercised authority may support a distinct power claim, subject to all its conditions. |
| A conqueror takes the facilities | Facilities follow the conqueror under accepted ownership rules. Foreign operation needs consent; past authorship is not erased solely by conquest. Loss of function, foreign/domestic classification and contender survival still matter. |
| A late patron pays the final ordinary invoice or reimburses old construction | Do not grant authorship of the entire program or retroactive shared membership. |
| A new financier accepts a genuinely substantial unfinished responsibility and completes it quickly | Can qualify under real accepted terms. No arbitrary timer; no credit before actual delivery. |
| A completed-role participant does no further work while others expand the undertaking | Preserve earned role protection and any explicit continuing duties. Do not add a participation-decay meter or move the personal target. |
| The new partner is much larger, richer or later spends more | Do not dilute an already earned contribution simply because the comparison changed. Collective scale remains separately assessed. |

For power, inspect actual current authority over affected operations separately from creation history and share ownership. Owning 25% of profits does not mechanically mean directing 25% of production; a ceremonial veto is not exercised operational authority over everything. Genuine shared operational authority can be recognized where concrete fulfilled responsibilities establish it. Its collective coverage is still a union, and mutually incompatible allocations cannot both be counted as delivered. Do not use a forced shutdown or a policy-toggle quota as proof.

## Player experience

Show the result and a short explanation in the existing economic/Legacy view:

> Kestrel's industrial region is operating. Your transport program supports the new production. Brin established the specialized integration for its eastern works. Kestrel operates the facilities.

For the explicit shared example, show each participant's actual promised role, delivered status and continuing duties, plus the undertaking's unique functioning reach. For ordinary commerce, do not ask the player to select credit percentages or register each contribution. Surface attribution automatically from actual orders and results. Use one clear shared-participation decision when that is being negotiated, not a permission request on each shipment.

Known major plans should explain expected contribution and why a proposed shared role is sufficient before commitment. Forecasts remain conditional on functioning delivery. Do not reveal private acts or hidden world totals through attribution explanations. Detailed evidence is inspectable when useful; routine invoicing, matching and aggregation remain automatic.

Gameplay: specialist, organizer, builder and financier can matter without a biggest-spender contest. Fun: receiving substantial help need not erase one's accomplishment. Excitement: a valuable unfinished role can enable a comeback or make cooperation strategically attractive, without selling last-minute honorary victory. Playability: finite concrete responsibilities and actual affected places replace player-managed fractions. Lovability: the story can remember several real contributors while distinguishing the current owner and operator.

## Recommendation and evidence limit

Accept the distinction between overlapping complementary contributions and subdivisions of the same work. Keep world extent, contribution evidence and winner eligibility separate. Preserve the existing fixed-role and independent/shared rules; do not add a credit currency, participation timer, new personal threshold or final vote.

Eight arithmetic checks verified the scope union, overlap, duplicate evidence, split finance, combined roles and function-loss cases. Independent 20%/18% claims and the collective 32% extent were also checked against the trial total threshold. These checks validate arithmetic given the stipulated contribution links. They do not validate actual causal attribution, finance availability, AI decisions, generated-map opportunities or enjoyment. Candidate-specific causal predicates and pooled-finance accounting still need explicit representation; they are not reasons to leave the proposed player-facing rule ambiguous.
