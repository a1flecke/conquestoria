# Commercial Legacy: cost, vulnerability and worthwhile decisions

Advisory investigation for issue #1125, developed 25 October 2026 and published as a planning snapshot on 26 October at the user's request. This develops the accepted opportunity/power measures. The user's subsequent “Agreed” accepts the design direction summarized below. No implementation. Calculations are constructed sensitivity checks, not game simulations, observed player behavior or calibrated balance.

## Accepted direction

Retain opportunity creation and economic power as distinct complete achievements, evaluated automatically with their existing trial thresholds. Balance actual development, integration, capacity, access and negotiated concessions; repeated sabotage or customer loss must not be the balancing foundation. Successful customer independence can preserve opportunity while reducing power when actual authority ends. Standing operating arrangements can exercise consequential authority without repeated player interventions.

Apply the failure boundaries below: nominal ownership, incidental supply, circular transactions and repeated reconstruction do not manufacture scope. Real funded subsidies remain legitimate; no minimum-profit or reserve rule is added. Completed stages must change useful capabilities, with routine execution automated and material consequences explained through legitimately known information. Preserve existing contribution, consent, continuity and survival requirements.

This acceptance covers the product direction and evaluation boundaries. It does not establish calibrated costs, equal difficulty, precise attribution across complex supply chains, empirical enjoyment or implementation readiness. Opportunity 30/20 and power 40/20 remain trial values. The original comparison and recommendation wording below are retained as rationale.

## Recommended decision

Keep opportunity and power as two complete achievements, evaluated automatically, with the accepted 30/20 and 40/20 trial values. Balance their ordinary economic work and meaningful concessions before relying on vulnerability. Do not make one cheap because opponents are expected to repeatedly sabotage it. Preserve completed opportunity through successful local independence; require actual continuing consequential authority for power. Both need functioning results under the existing continuity rules.

Three alternatives considered:

1. Equalize percentages or add an opaque combined effort score. Reject: equal percentages do not mean equal work, and an effort score obscures the accomplishment.
2. Make power much cheaper but compensate with frequent loss of customers, sabotage or compulsory renewals. Reject as the balancing foundation: it rewards denial spam, disadvantages peaceful games and makes the experience depend on other players repeatedly policing the leader.
3. Let different real opportunities determine the attractive approach, charging actual development, integration, capacity, access and conceded returns. Recommend. Vulnerability remains consequential without becoming mandatory recurring maintenance.

This resolves the intended balance mechanism. It does not establish final costs, fair finish dates or numerical parity.

## Compare the actual work, not the displayed percentage

Hold the starting world at 100 and give the same contender 10 domestic units of already established qualifying power, no qualifying opportunity, and the same treasury, development capacity, technologies and relationships in either strategy. Those existing 10 are a declared starting advantage for power, not a free benefit added only after choosing that strategy. Both strategies must secure actual counterpart consent and supported operating variety.

If opportunity is entirely additional productive activity, its threshold requires x/(100+x) >= 0.30: 42.86 new extent, with at least 28.57 foreign. For power, exercising authority over another 30 existing foreign extent reaches 40/100 overall and 30/100 foreign. This comparison assumes power reorganization adds no output; real added output must also enter the reference. An all-new opportunity model is an analytical case, not a requirement that every opportunity be net global output growth.

Normalize development work per opportunity extent to one. Then:

| Work per added power extent, relative to opportunity | Opportunity work | Additional power work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 | 42.86 | 15 |
| 1.0 | 42.86 | 30 |
| 1.5 | 42.86 | 45 |
| 2.0 | 42.86 | 60 |

The break-even ratio is 1.43 in this starting position. It is not a prescribed multiplier. With no already qualifying power, power needs 40 extent and the ratio is only 1.07. Starting assets matter substantially. At opportunity targets of 25% and 35%, the first comparison's break-even ratios become 1.11 and 1.79. Threshold changes are powerful; do not use them to conceal missing economic costs.

Work alone is not cost. Cash expenditure, earlier net receipts, continuing obligations, surrendered profits, travel, prerequisites and displaced functions must also be compared. Buying a contractor can reduce national work without reducing expenditure. Retaining a profitable route can finance a slower expansion. Use actual affordable schedules as in the earlier build/buy/redirect trial; do not translate these work units into claimed turn counts or invent equal prices to manufacture parity.

## Make each route worth playing before its finish

Opportunity's satisfying action is removing a substantial commercial constraint: reliable access, supply or investment makes real production feasible. Its costs can include building capacity, developing expertise, supplying inputs and sharing returns so the recipient wants the arrangement. Its reward is useful functioning places, income or access where terms provide them, and historical contribution that need not depend on perpetual customer dependence.

Power's satisfying action is organizing and directing a consequential operating economy: allocation of real capacity, supply commitments, investment and access materially shape functioning activity. Its costs can include integrating operations, acquiring appropriate assets, retaining competence, financing the service and accepting terms that preserve counterpart agency. It can be a trusted, abundant supplier. Extortion and artificial scarcity are optional hostile behavior, not qualification requirements.

Neither is a mandatory class, ideology, project catalog or economic stance. Investment, production and network organization can serve both. Neither has a fixed obligation to cost more in every world. A large existing integrated economy may favor power; accessible constraints and willing recipients may favor opportunity. A recipient can refuse either, especially a late agreement that knowingly concedes an exclusive defeat. No guaranteed market or compulsory final endorsement is added.

## Counterplay and near-finish stress checks

Use still-unfinished claims so no awarded victory or fully earned protected package is revoked. Opportunity begins at O=40, Of=30, R=140. Power begins at P=38, Pf=28, R=100. These are separate constructed campaign outcomes from a world initially at 100, not two contradictory measurements of one simultaneous world.

| Change before qualification | Opportunity consequence | Power consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No disruption | Needs 2.86 additional qualifying new foreign extent | Needs 2 additional qualifying existing foreign extent |
| A foreign customer representing 12 becomes independently capable; the achieved productive function continues | O=40 remains; no penalty for successful development | If the contender genuinely loses authority over those 12, P=26 and Pf=16; needs 14 additional existing foreign extent |
| A required opportunity function of 12 is genuinely lost | O=28, Of=18; at reference 140 its share is 20% | Actual authority over nonfunctioning activity cannot count merely because its agreement survives |
| Unrelated production grows by 20 | O=40/R160=25%; needs 11.43 additional new foreign extent | P=38/R120=31.67%; needs 10 additional existing foreign extent |

The function-loss case holds the reference at 140 under existing decline normalization. Existing continuity rules govern temporary disruption; it is not a new instantaneous cancellation policy. Repairing the lost function is different from building entirely new scope and need not enlarge the reference in the same way.

Conclusions: opportunity has a real durability advantage against commercial displacement, but not immunity to functional failure or unrelated world growth. Power has more exposure to replacement of authority. Neither requires a permanent customer grievance, forced war, scripted sabotage or an arbitrary holding period to be interesting. A rival can advance its own achievement, build competing capability, replace a real operating relationship where allowed, or disrupt actual dependencies. Genuine successful local development does not become sabotage of the creator's historical accomplishment.

## A mixed campaign that changes direction without resetting

Consider one internally consistent mixed state: R=140, O=40, Of=30, P=54 and Pf=40. Opportunity and power each fail their respective total threshold. Their overlapping activity is not summed. A possible composition has 40 new extent (10 domestic, 30 foreign), of which 34 is also under qualifying authority (4 domestic, 30 foreign), plus 20 pre-existing extent under authority (10 domestic, 10 foreign).

Kestrel takes over independent operation of 12 foreign extent whose productive capability the contender helped establish. The function remains, but this example stipulates that the contender's actual authority there ends. O stays 40 while P falls to 42 and Pf to 28. The player need not fight to restore dependency. If a legitimate next investment creates three additional qualifying foreign extent, the result is O=43, Of=33, R=143: 30.07% overall and 23.08% foreign. Opportunity extent qualifies. If those three are also under authority, P=45 and Pf=31; power still does not qualify. All other achievement requirements must also hold for a win.

If Kestrel instead retains the original operating arrangement and the same three are created, P becomes 57/R143=39.86%: still just short of power, while opportunity can already qualify. One more existing unit under genuine authority would make P=58 and both extent tests pass. The player need not finish that extra work once a complete opportunity victory is available. No up-front track choice, conversion fee, extra finale or forfeiture of previously established work is needed.

These outcomes validate the distinction logically. They do not establish that the extra investment is affordable, accepted or timely in an actual match. Its concrete availability must be visible through legitimate knowledge, not promised by a comeback script.

## Exploits and experience failures to exclude

| Failure | Recommended resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Buy a token stake, sign a memorandum, then claim the customer's entire economy | Paper rights and passive holdings are insufficient. Identify actual activity materially shaped by exercised authority and the contender's substantial contribution. Acquisition can qualify when it really establishes those facts; no categorical ban or artificial waiting timer. |
| Toggle a policy solely to prove that authority was exercised | Standing commitments and automatic operations can exercise authority through their real effects. No required number of interventions or staged crisis. |
| Deliver a small necessary input and claim every downstream industry | Bound extent to the supported effect. Identify the represented constraint, the capability supplied and the actual functioning extent. The smallest incidental input does not automatically inherit all downstream scope. |
| Several suppliers each inflate the same productive outcome | Count each affected activity once within a claim and once in the world reference. Contribution and winner eligibility remain separate; do not arbitrarily divide all credit by supplier count or award every supplier a victory. |
| Produce, destroy and rebuild the same capability repeatedly | Current functioning extent is a set of real activity, not cumulative construction or shipment totals. Restoration cannot multiply it. Existing decline and conquest rules still apply; no intent detector. |
| Dump unwanted output, cycle goods between friendly accounts, or grant money back and forth | No additional useful productive result or authority is established by the transaction itself. Genuine fulfilled demand, capacity and consequences matter. Friendly counterparties do not turn meaningless exchange into scope. |
| Require every enterprise to be individually profitable | Reject. Actual funded public investment, loss leaders and subsidies are allowed. Charge real obligations against real receipts and treasury. No credit for an unfunded paper plan or idle enterprise, but no added minimum-profit or reserve rule. |
| Buy an economy using an abstract victory button | Money can acquire real assets and services; available capacity, consent, expertise and substantial contribution remain necessary. Never add an arbitrary extra obstacle when all requirements already genuinely hold. |
| Transfer a facility and instantly erase its creator's history | Ownership and permitted operation follow the new owner. Historical contribution can remain if the achieved function continues; power depends on actual remaining authority. No continuing foreign operation without consent. |
| Repeated low-cost attacks create endless repair chores | Do not balance the path around constant disruption. Review attack cost, reach, recovery choices and recurrence in the shared espionage/combat systems. Avoid a new commerce-only immunity or compulsory protection-click cycle. |

The causality boundary must be explainable through represented facts, not a hidden alternate-history simulator. If a designer cannot say which substantial function changed and why this undertaking supports it, do not inflate the scope to make the percentages work. This gives concrete acceptance cases; exact attribution across complex mixed supply chains remains a technical/product representation issue, not solved by this paper.

## Other games: evidence and design inference

Offworld Trading Company's official gameplay description makes production, purchases, limited claims and spending choices consequential. Its stock buyout is a separate finale. Borrow the tradeoffs, not automatic conversion of wealth into Conquestoria victory. [Official gameplay](https://www.offworldgame.com/game/gameplay).

PC Gamer awarded Offworld 88/100 and praised the satisfaction of deliberately engineered profit. GameSpot praised adaptation and comeback options while finding eight-player sabotage difficult to track. These are specific reviewers' experiences, not a population-wide reception claim. Our inference: economic choices can be exciting, but escalating disruption and notifications can undermine playability. [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/offworld-trading-company-review/), [GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/offworld-trading-company-review/1900-6416420/).

Anno 1800's Docklands diary describes specialization and replacing production chains with imports, along with visible modular harbor development and recurring contract fulfillment. It also describes a trader with effectively unlimited stocks. Borrow visible transformation and persistent execution; that guaranteed external supply would bypass the recipient-agency and world-significance tests here. This is a mechanics comparison, not a claim about Docklands reception. [Official diary](https://www.anno-union.com/devblog-docklands/).

## Gameplay, fun, excitement, playability and lovability

- Gameplay: competing uses of real cash, capacity and relationships create decisions. Test whether each candidate option sacrifices something useful now, rather than merely adding an invisible future risk.
- Fun: completing an expansion changes production, available supplies, usable connections or an actual strategic choice. If only a progress bar changes, the example fails.
- Excitement: a competitor, changing opportunity or approaching finish can make a previously unattractive investment or redeployment worthwhile. Do not manufacture a crisis at fixed intervals or require a response where the player has no real options.
- Playability: approve a comprehensible operating plan, then automate routine work. Notify when known facts materially change function, viability or an impending finish; combine repeated symptoms of one cause. Do not require shipment tagging, annual renewals or repeated progress proofs.
- Lovability: preserve identifiable places, assets and relationships. A former customer becoming an independent producer can be a proud outcome and a new competitor. Show the consequence and provenance through existing map/advisor/history surfaces instead of creating a new administrative layer.

Example notification, only when these facts are legitimately known: “Kestrel now operates the works independently. The industry you helped establish continues; you no longer direct its supply allocations.” Explain income and obligations separately if those actually changed. Do not infer private global victory totals or a hidden rival deadline.

## Decision boundary and verification

Recommend retaining both achievements and the accepted trial thresholds, using real development/integration costs and concessions as the balance foundation, and carrying the exploit boundaries above into candidate evaluation. Customer independence is valid counterplay to power and can be a success for opportunity. Do not compensate with mandatory sabotage, rent-seeking, final customer approval or repetitive maintenance.

Arithmetic checked: threshold sensitivity at 25/30/35; four relative-work cases; seven near-finish/disruption states; five mixed-network states. Checks asserted the key growth and qualification outcomes. The earlier build/buy/redirect budget remains a separate checked scheduling example and is not evidence for these new campaigns' affordability. No game engine, AI, generated-world or player tests were run. Final economic coefficients, precise complex-chain attribution and empirical balance remain unresolved. This pass resolves the recommended product mechanism and concrete failure boundaries, not implementation readiness.
