# Commercial financing: attach credit to delivered provision

Advisory design record for issue #1125, published as a planning snapshot on 26 October 2026 at the user's request. The user's “Approved” accepts the financing refinement summarized below. No implementation, calibrated costs or gameplay simulation. Preserve the accepted opportunity/power distinction, fixed substantial roles, funded-budget limits, automatic operation, continuity, consent and independent/shared victory conditions.

## Approved financing refinement

Attribute financing to the actual provision it funded, using delivery boundaries that correspond to real work. Do not redistribute the whole undertaking's history according to each civilization's eventual share of total spending. A late expensive rescue must not purchase credit for earlier delivered work; efficiency must not reduce credit for the same complete result.

Keep promised results fixed and funding bounded. Before a material funding change is accepted, explain if it would leave a finance-only role incomplete; do not silently reinterpret that role as organizing or grant a rescuer automatic shared eligibility. Routine funding and delivery remain automatic. Preserve the distinctions among unused funds, delivered work, functioning outcomes, historical contribution and winner eligibility. Equivalent cheaper delivery can fulfill the same result; ordinary later returns do not erase authorship, and reimbursement does not transfer it.

Approval settles this product refinement. The numbers remain illustrative; indivisible pooled provision, intermediation, affordability, timing and empirical balance still require validation or representation-specific rules. The original alternatives and recommendation wording below are retained as rationale.

Three approaches:

- Lifetime cash shares: simple calculation, but price changes and late spending reassign earlier accomplishments. Reject.
- Freeze all attribution when funds are promised: predictable, but unused promises and unrealized budgets become credit. Reject.
- Record actual financed provision as it is delivered, retain pending evidence for unfinished functions, and require the promised functioning outcome for fulfillment. Recommend.

Do not add a player-managed construction accounting layer. Existing actual plans, deliveries, capacity and authorized funding should supply the evidence automatically. The analytical boundaries below are not extra turn-by-turn choices.

## A concrete two-application case

Two real industrial applications each support 30 units of qualifying activity. Each can function independently once its actual requirements are met. They are different operating applications, not artificial subdivisions of one indivisible facility. Together they contribute 60 unique extent to a larger undertaking; neither this example nor the number 60 establishes a complete world-scale Legacy.

Auren and Brin each accept a finance-only responsibility for one complete 30-unit application, with an initial cost estimate and authorized budget of 90. Assume both roles were legitimately substantial for their agreed undertaking; this is not a new role threshold. In this deliberately narrow example neither has another substantial developer or organizer contribution that can substitute for financing it did not provide.

Auren's 90 finances the first application, which is delivered and functions. Its 30-unit contribution is fulfilled under its actual terms. Before any provision for the second is delivered, a known supply problem doubles that application's cost from 90 to 180. This is a stipulated economic shock, not a proposed random tax or compulsory crisis. No automatic extra spending or borrowing occurs.

### Three possible responses

| Response, if genuinely available and authorized | Actual result | Financing attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brin funds the additional 90 and completes the application | Same 30-unit result, at greater actual cost | Auren 30; Brin 30 |
| Brin and a new backer, Cera, each finance 90 of the second application's 180 provision | Same 30-unit result, jointly financed | Auren 30; Brin 15; Cera 15 |
| A competent replacement delivers the same complete function for 60 | Same 30-unit result, less spending; 30 of Brin's original budget remains unused | Auren 30; Brin 30 |

The equal split in the middle row is valid because both parties finance equal shares of the same as-yet-undelivered provision at the same actual terms. It is not a formula for assigning all historical credit from cumulative gold flows. Delivered work and functioning scope must actually follow the financial provision before the numbers become realized credit.

In the rescue branch, each civilization spent 90, so a lifetime-spending formula would assign 20 to each across the total 60. That would reduce Auren's fulfilled 30 to 20 because somebody else's later work became expensive. The delivered-provision rule instead retains 30/15/15. It also prevents Cera's late payment from acquiring part of Auren's past achievement.

Under the cheaper replacement, Brin's contribution remains 30, even though its spending is lower than Auren's. An equivalent delivered result matters more than matching a bill. The replacement must actually have the competence, access, inputs and available capacity; a cheaper quote is not proof of feasibility or equal completion time.

## The uncomfortable but necessary boundary

In the rescue branch, Brin promised to finance the complete 30-unit application but actually financed half. Its finance-only role is not automatically fulfilled. Its target did not rise: the means of delivering it became more costly, and another civilization supplied part of the provision.

Warn Brin before agreeing to the changed plan. It can fund its original delivery if affordable; choose a genuinely equivalent cheaper provision; negotiate a legitimate amendment to an unfinished substantial responsibility; or accept that this financial role will remain incomplete. An amendment cannot retroactively pretend Brin supplied Cera's contribution or waive substantial-role requirements. Do not silently assign extra tasks elsewhere, increase the original target or borrow money.

This is distinct from an operator or organizer whose already defined substantial role was to establish the complete function using purchased services and financing. Such a role is judged by its actual promised responsibility and substantial work; it need not personally perform every delivery. Do not reclassify an existing finance-only role as organizing after the result is complete to manufacture qualification.

Cera does not automatically become a shared winner. Its actual remaining role must be sufficient, explicitly agreed before completion and genuinely fulfilled under the existing rules. If the remaining work is too small, ordinary paid assistance remains possible, but a shared winner seat is unavailable. No minimum membership duration or waiting timer is added. A legitimate late substantial role can succeed quickly.

The undertaking may finish for eligible participants even if Brin never fulfills its role, according to the accepted common-checkpoint rules. A rescue is not a guarantee that every original participant wins. This consequence belongs in the material-change preview, not in a surprise ending message.

## Pooled funds, unfinished work and indivisible functions

A pool is a means of payment, not a new source of scope. Its actual draw must follow the agreed backing of the provision it buys. A member's unused deposit creates no realized extent. Funding the same delivery through several internal transfers does not create several financial contributions. Combining a funding link with a separate substantial technical contribution still uses unique coverage within that civilization's claim.

For construction that cannot function in stages, retain evidence of genuinely delivered work without marking the economic application active or the complete role fulfilled. A half-built bridge does not create half its intended operating economy. When the complete function starts, recognize its actual contributors and supported extent. Failure or abandonment does not turn spending into a successful legacy.

Do not impose the two-application example's 30/30 allocation on indivisible designs without a real basis. Where separate delivered responsibilities exist, use them. Where participants genuinely cofinance the same indivisible provision, apply their actual funded shares of that provision, while respecting prior independently fulfilled contributions. Cost changes can affect whether an unfinished bounded budget will deliver its promised share. They must not retroactively alter a completed contribution or silently enlarge its fixed promise.

Unfinished contribution forecasts are conditional. The offer must distinguish an estimate of what a budget can buy from a guaranteed sufficiency of the promised result. “Deliver this complete function and your role is fulfilled” is compatible with uncertain procurement cost. “Spend 90 and you qualify regardless of what happens” is not.

Do not add arbitrary construction phases just to generate early credit. Actual physically useful stages, genuine delivered responsibilities and valid candidate-specific measures must determine boundaries. If an indivisible candidate cannot explain its delivered contribution coherently, its finance role needs more specification before it is offered as a reliable victory commitment; a hidden gold formula is not an acceptable substitute.

## Refunds, returns and replacements

| Event | Attribution consequence |
| --- | --- |
| A contributor reserves 90 but nothing is delivered | No realized operating scope from the reservation. Actual committed execution can still fix the promised role under the existing commencement rule. |
| The same function is delivered for 60 and the unused 30 is released | Full legitimate delivered result remains; unused funds never contributed additional extent. |
| A supplier returns an unused deposit after cancellation | Remove the unused backing; do not erase separately delivered useful work or claim the canceled delivery. |
| An operating investment later earns revenue or a legitimate loan is repaid | Ordinary returns do not erase the historical provision it actually financed. Do not continually recalculate authorship from the current outstanding cash balance. This does not introduce a loan system or authorize automatic debt. |
| Cera reimburses Auren after Auren's application already works | Ownership or financial terms may change if agreed; past authorship does not transfer with the payment. |
| Replacement equipment maintains the same achieved function | Apply existing succession/continuity rules; no automatic reset or new copy of the same extent. |
| Work is destroyed and financed again | Actual recovery can matter, but current extent is counted once; destruction and rebuilding cannot multiply it. |
| Several civilizations provide routine continuing subsidies | Charge real funding and preserve real operation. Repeated payments do not repeatedly add the same operating capacity to historical scope. |
| A higher invoice is circulated through friendly accounts | No additional provision or scope follows from price or transfer volume alone. Actual delivery and backed responsibilities remain the evidence. |

Financial intermediation, loans and mixed in-kind procurement must not create repeated credit for one delivery. Their exact accounting needs the eventual economy's supported actions; do not invent a full banking simulator to settle this candidate. The proposed guardrail is that every claimed financing share must reconcile to actual delivered provision without transferring already earned authorship or creating duplicate extent.

## Other games: borrow useful execution, avoid importing chores

Anno 1800's original World Fair design uses construction phases and gives the completed monument a further gameplay use through exhibitions. The transferable lesson is visible delivery and a useful result. Our functional stages must be real; this is not a reason to require every commercial undertaking to be a monument or to import its detailed production pipelines. [Official World Fair diary](https://www.anno-union.com/devblog-welcome-to-the-world-fair/).

Factorio describes blueprints and construction robots as defining what to build, supplying materials and letting robots carry out construction. Borrow authorization followed by automatic routine execution. Its developers also explain that removing small hassles leaves players more room to enjoy new systems, and discuss frustrating robot dispatch delays. That supports making actual bottlenecks understandable instead of asking the player to repair the automation through repeated clicks. These are mechanics and developer observations, not reception statistics or evidence that this Conquestoria rule is fun. [Official content description](https://www.factorio.com/game/content), [Smarter robots developer article](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-374).

## What the player sees

Before commitment: the named result, its actual benefit, the role's fixed delivery, authorized funding, estimate and material dependencies. No separate attribution slider.

When circumstances materially change, one actionable explanation:

> The second industrial application now needs 90 additional funding. Your promised result is unchanged. You can authorize the extra funding, choose the available equivalent supplier, or consider Cera's cofinancing offer. Cofinancing would change which part you personally financed and may leave your current role incomplete; review that consequence before accepting. Auren's completed contribution is unaffected.

Actual options must be present, affordable where indicated, and permitted. The example cannot promise a replacement supplier or willing rescuer in every world. An already authorized equivalent substitution can execute automatically. Changes to budget, promised outcome, foreign rights or shared participation require the relevant informed decision. Routine draws and deliveries do not.

Functioning applications should show changed production, supply and useful possibilities on existing surfaces. A funding-progress notification without a useful result is insufficient. The player should remember the industry rescued, the capacity redirected or the partner who helped, rather than the sequence of invoices.

## Recommendation and validation boundary

Adopt delivered-provision attribution and reject lifetime-spending redistribution. Keep fixed promised results, explicit bounded budgets, truthful conditional forecasts, efficiency rewards and a clear distinction between rescue and earned shared eligibility. This is a refinement of the accepted attribution rule, not another victory path or new financial subsystem.

Six simple funding cases were calculated, with conservation checks confirming that allocated financing extent sums to the provision in each case. The rescue branch was compared with the incorrect 20/20/20 lifetime-cash split; its supported result is 30/15/15. No scheduling, completion-time, AI, market or player simulation was run. Prices and capacity are stipulated. Indivisible pooled provision and intermediation require representation-specific rules before implementation; they must not silently be treated as solved by the modular arithmetic example.
