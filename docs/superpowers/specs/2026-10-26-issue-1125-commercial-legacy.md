## Accepted accomplishment

Commercial Legacy can culminate in substantial newly enabled productive capability that actually functions, or substantial world activity shaped by exercised economic decisions. Players can combine production, investment, supply and organization without choosing an exclusive track. Evaluate both complete achievements automatically; two incomplete claims do not combine into a third weighted-score victory.

A large treasury, high profit, ownership label, shipment count, transit through a foreign port or first monopoly on a tiny resource is insufficient. Power does not require harmful coercion, political obedience, customer collapse or deliberately manufactured scarcity. Opportunity can emerge in a prosperous economy when a real constraint is removed. Neither achievement requires one designated megaproject.

Both need world significance, actual substantial contribution, meaningful operating variety, current required functions and survival. These requirements are conjunctive. Percentages alone never award a victory.

## Common economic reference and trial extent

Use actual qualifying activity and a whole-world productive reference with consistent comparison values. Remove repeated intermediate-output counting; transaction prices, price inflation and prestige multipliers do not manufacture scope. Actual prices still govern real economic choices. Exact coefficients and mixed-chain representation remain unsettled.

Opportunity trial: at least 30% of the world reference overall, including at least 20% of the world reference in other independent economies. Power trial: 40% overall, including the same 20% foreign floor. Foreign extent is a subset of total extent. These replace the earlier uniform 40/20 opportunity proposal; they are accepted trial values, not equal-difficulty findings.

New productive activity enters the denominator as well as any valid numerator. Starting from 100 with no qualifying opportunity, entirely new qualifying production needs about 42.86 to reach 30% of the resulting 142.86 world. Its own qualifying growth increases its share; it does not move progress backward. Unrelated growth can change an incomplete claim's relative reach, subject to existing earned-package protection. Do not freeze a smaller world reference by an early declaration.

Finite normalization after devastation is accepted: trial 50 world turns early, 30 middle and 20 late, speed-scaled. A public campaign schedule assigns duration at the decline, with gradual transitions; a later era change does not shorten an existing interval. Separate depths of decline retain separate persistence; minor fluctuations cannot restart everything. A conqueror or aggressor can eventually benefit from the normalized smaller world, but destruction itself supplies no achievement credit. Exact schedule boundaries, rounding and update order remain to specify.

Hidden activity affects real qualification without leaking through exact percentages, countdowns, recommendations or viability labels. Show legitimate known facts and appropriately uncertain global estimates. Fixed shared-role sufficiency can be explained for a concrete offered delivery without disclosing hidden world totals or private conduct.

## Availability, consent and independent play

The user explicitly accepts that international commercial Legacy may be unsuitable for some matches, including some duels. Do not automatically disable every duel, invalidate an established achievement when two civilizations remain, or invent guaranteed neutral customers. Ordinary trade remains useful without a credible Legacy finish.

Compact civilizations can establish substantial activity beyond their borders through real capabilities and agreements, without conquering every customer or making every supplier a co-winner. They are not entitled to foreign consent. Useful initial opportunities, intelligible constraints and genuine alternatives where the setup supports them are the design target; permanent cooperation is not guaranteed.

Four markets behind one strait may have one gatekeeper. Count dependencies, not just flags: transport, access, expertise, essential inputs, finance and services can share a controller. Four small friendly markets may still be too small. An undiscovered opportunity is not automatically absent, and an available route is not a willing recipient.

Counterparts have their own ambitions and legitimate information. A profitable arrangement can be unattractive when it knowingly concedes an exclusive defeat. Personality, values and difficulty influence judgments; no omniscient universal boycott or compulsory acceptance. Early useful exchange and genuinely shared ambitions can support cooperation. Existing completed work does not need a new final endorsement to count.

## Finance and operation

Temporary deficits are allowed when actual treasury reserves cover them. A zero treasury is not itself failure when receipts fund obligations. No automatic debt, invented future money, extra reserve floor or per-project profitability requirement. Public investment and funded subsidies can be valid. Lost operating receipts must be included when comparing cuts or substitutions.

Use one understandable national plan with actual authority and bounded funding. Preserve functions and pause expansion by default when funding fails, while allowing an informed override. Routine operations and allowed substitutions execute automatically; material changes to budget, result, rights or shared participation need the relevant informed decision. No annual invoices, local-policy chores or oscillating automatic stop/restart loop.

Build new capacity, purchase genuinely available services, redirect suitable assets, or combine them. Count travel, conversion, displaced functions and foregone income. A transferred fleet cannot retain its old service automatically. A provider can refuse or quote too much; hiring does not grant a host's operating permission.

A prior constructed recovery schedule illustrates these accepted choices. Starting at turn 5 with treasury 62, two national work capacity and the stated available provider/fleet, building reached the trial on turn 14 with 56 cash; hiring on turn 11 with 6; redirecting on turn 11 with 19; combining on turn 10 with 6. These are different-time balances, not equal-time returns. With 40 cash needed for another actual commitment, all approached turn 14 and retaining the old income favored building. The figures depend on stipulated delivery costs, permissions and equipment; they do not prove typical availability or final balance.

## Contribution, scope and ownership

Count each affected functioning activity once within a claim and the world reference. Several substantial complementary contributors can be recognized over the same result without diluting one another. Splitting the same financing or throughput cannot award each participant credit for the whole delivery. Different role names cannot manufacture complementary work.

Historical authorship, contribution-linked coverage, actual current authority and winner eligibility remain distinct. A customer becoming independently capable can preserve the originating opportunity while reducing power if authority ends. A failed enterprise does not keep current scope merely because a delivery was fulfilled. Actual replacement and succession follow the continuing-function rules; obsolete equipment need not be preserved.

Ownership can change through purchase or conquest without purchasing past authorship. Captured facilities belong to the conqueror; foreign operation requires consent, and operation can depend on capability. A new owner can establish its own genuine power or opportunity contribution without an arbitrary waiting timer, but nominal ownership is not sufficient evidence.

## Shared roles and financing refinement

A shared role must be explicitly admitted before it is completed, promise a substantial concrete result and enter real authorized execution with actual backing. Its promised result then stays fixed. World growth, routine substitutions or additional partners cannot silently increase it. Costs can change; bounded resources and actual fulfillment still matter. Ordinary paid suppliers are not automatically shared winners, while payment does not erase genuine authorship or prevent a complete independent claim.

Do not use a continuously rising personal world percentage. Earlier 10%-of-world admission sizing was a trial, not a universal release threshold. Name the actual complete responsibility and disclose sufficiency under its terms. Unfinished members cannot hold up the whole qualifying finish; late participants can qualify through genuinely substantial remaining work without an arbitrary membership timer. A final tip or reimbursement cannot create honorary eligibility.

Financing credit follows delivered provision. A late expensive rescue cannot redistribute credit for earlier completed work through lifetime spending ratios. Equivalent cheaper delivery fulfills the same result. Unused reserves create no delivered credit; normal profits and repayments do not erase historical provision. Indivisible unfinished facilities do not produce operating extent merely because funds were spent.

If a finance-only participant promised to fund a whole application but a changed arrangement leaves it funding only half, warn that its role can remain incomplete before accepting the change. Do not retrospectively relabel that role as organizing or automatically grant the rescuer a shared seat. A genuinely different organizer role is judged by its actual substantial responsibility. Detailed pooled-finance/intermediation rules remain representation-dependent.

## Experience evidence and open work

Other games inform bounded lessons: Offworld offers context-dependent production and purchase choices; Guild Wars 2 motivates non-diluting cooperation; Age of Wonders 4 provides a membership-shortcut warning; Victoria 3 separates ownership relationships from actual economic activity; Factorio supports automated execution and reducing small hassles. Supporting sources and their limits are linked in the detailed documents. Developer design notes are not reception statistics, and individual reviews are not population-wide findings.

The latest campaign experiment checks affordable serial schedules. Its baseline reached opportunity and power on turn 16, with mixed power on turn 15. Different cash and preparation favored different approaches. A turn-6 Kestrel operating transition left opportunity finishing on turn 16, a replanned mixed strategy on turn 18 and the concentrated power recovery on turn 24. A turn-18 rival would beat the last recovery. Twenty-four selected schedules were replayed for arithmetic consistency. Those are authored economic assumptions, not actual match outcomes or proof of fun.

The campaign's proposed conclusion is to preserve flexibility and explain real exposure, while requiring useful world consequences instead of purchase-and-wait progress. That recommendation has not yet been explicitly accepted. No guaranteed rescue, mandatory recurring sabotage or new attribution subsystem follows.

Remaining work includes precise qualifying activity/authority predicates, unlike-output valuation, indivisible pooled provision, realistic costs/rates, ordinary world availability, recipient and AI incentives, normalization timing details, information-safe previews and playable experience validation. The accepted product boundaries are stable enough to stop adding speculative accounting rules; these unresolved parts must not be presented as implemented or calibrated.
