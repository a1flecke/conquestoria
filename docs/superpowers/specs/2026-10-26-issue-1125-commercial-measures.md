# Commercial opportunity and power: distinct achievement measures

Advisory design record for issue #1125, developed 25 October 2026 and published as a planning snapshot on 26 October at the user's request. The user accepted the direction below with “Agreed” following the comparison of opportunity creation and economic power. No implementation. Numerical thresholds are accepted for trials, not calibrated balance; arithmetic checks are not gameplay simulation.

## Accepted direction and trial values — 25 October 2026

Use two distinct complete achievement tests against the same world reference. Opportunity measures substantial newly enabled productive capability that is actually functioning. Power measures substantial current productive activity demonstrably shaped by the contender's exercised economic decisions. Money, transactions, ownership labels and ordinary economic growth do not establish either achievement by themselves.

For the next comparison, test 30% overall extent for opportunity and retain the existing trial of 40% for power. Keep the same 20%-of-world foreign minimum for both. Do not use a frozen smaller world reference, subtract the claimant's contribution from the denominator, or create a weighted combined score.

These values are a deliberately provisional comparison, not a claim that the two expressions have equal difficulty. The accepted measurement architecture uses distinct evidence, a common reference, substantial foreign extent and no double counting. Costs, dependencies, continuity and actual play must calibrate the numbers.

Players can combine opportunity creation and economic power without choosing a track upfront. Evaluate both complete achievement tests automatically; satisfying either can qualify. Two incomplete claims do not combine into a third blended-score victory. The same activity may support evidence for both tests, but counts only once within each claim. The existing substantive achievement, operating variety, contribution, continuity and survival requirements still apply.

## What each achievement actually proves

| | Opportunity creation | Economic power |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defining result | Consequential productive possibilities or expanded feasible operating scale became real through a substantial contribution | Consequential decisions currently shape substantial functioning economic activity |
| Example | Reliable access and productive equipment remove a represented constraint, enabling additional industry that actually operates | A functioning production, supply or investment organization exercises material allocation decisions across substantial activity |
| Extent | The attributable new or newly enabled functioning activity, bounded by the real change | The actual activity subject to the demonstrated consequential authority |
| Customer becomes independently capable | The originator's contribution can remain while the enabled result functions | Assess what decision authority remains; historical influence does not substitute for current power |
| Required result stops working | The affected current opportunity extent no longer counts, subject to existing continuity rules | Actual lost functions or authority reduce the affected extent under the existing rules |
| Not sufficient | Funding an unused facility, announcing potential output, ordinary sales, or taking credit for unrelated growth | High profits, passive asset holdings, being sole producer of a tiny commodity, or claiming all of a customer's economy |

Opportunity is not simply a country's observed GDP increase. If an industry grows from 60 to 100, the candidate opportunity extent is the attributable increment, at most 40 in that example—not automatically all 100, and not automatically all 40 if other independent changes caused some of the growth. Evidence must follow supported constraints, capabilities and operating results. No hidden simulated alternate history or player-assigned shipment attribution is required.

The whole world's change in output and the scope of a newly enabled function are different facts. Other production can grow or decline independently. Do not make net global GDP growth the universal opportunity predicate. Any case involving changed productive capabilities rather than additional output must establish its concrete substantive change; a new customer name, ownership change or small efficiency improvement cannot stand in for it.

Power does not require extortion, monopoly, political obedience or demonstrating that customers collapse without the contender. A reliable supplier can exercise substantial decisions. But owning a profit share or selling one necessary minor input does not automatically confer authority over every downstream activity. Acquiring assets can support a new achievement; it does not transfer another civilization's historical authorship or waive the buyer's own substantial-contribution requirement.

## Common reference and retained rules

Let R be the applicable whole-world productive reference, measured with the same stable comparison values used for extent. It includes foreign and unknown activity and genuine new production. Existing finite normalization, continuity, information, survival and common-checkpoint rules remain unchanged. No new reference clock, declaration date, era floor or holding period is proposed.

Let O be qualifying opportunity extent and Of its foreign subset. Let P be qualifying power extent and Pf its foreign subset. The proposed numerical components are:

- Opportunity: O / R >= 30% AND Of / R >= 20%.
- Power: P / R >= 40% AND Pf / R >= 20%.

Each also requires its complete substantive result, substantial contender contribution, meaningful operating variety and required current functions. These equations alone do not award a victory. Foreign is actual affected activity in another independent economy, not the registration of a company or transit through a foreign port. Domestic plus foreign forms the total; foreign is not added twice.

Keeping the foreign minimum unchanged deliberately preserves international substance. At the exact opportunity threshold, at least two-thirds of counted extent must be foreign; at the exact power threshold, at least half must be foreign. Those proportions are consequences of the two world-share floors, not additional fixed ratio requirements. Larger overall claims still need the same foreign world-share floor. This does not change the accepted Ocean regional-distribution rule or import it into commerce.

The measures remain automatic. The player need not declare an exclusive track, assign qualifying shipments, select a valuation date or click a claim-confirmation button. Match-dependent viability, including unsuitable duels, remains accepted.

## Correct the growth explanation

Our earlier warning identified a real difference in magnitude but could imply that the player's successful growth reduces progress. It does not. With no other changes, adding genuinely qualifying output increases O / R whenever O < R. For example, 30 / 130 = 23.1% becomes 40 / 140 = 28.6%.

The issue is what scale a chosen world-share threshold demands. Starting from a world of 100 with no existing qualifying opportunity, and assuming all added production qualifies and no other output changes:

| Opportunity world-share threshold | Required new productive extent | Resulting world reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25% | 33.33 | 133.33 |
| 30% | 42.86 | 142.86 |
| 35% | 53.85 | 153.85 |
| 40% | 66.67 | 166.67 |

The relationship is x / (100 + x). Thirty percent is a useful test anchor because even this favorable illustration requires a transformation exceeding 40% of the original world's productive scale. It does not make opportunity trivially small. Forty percent of an unchanged world under exercised economic authority is also substantial, but neither this comparison nor the similar absolute magnitudes proves equal effort or risk.

Opportunity can retain its originator's contribution after customer independence, while power can require continuing authority. That durability may offset some creation cost. Conversely, building new capability may be more expensive or require more adaptation than organizing existing activity. Test these practical differences before calling 30/40 balanced. Retain 25 and 35 as sensitivity comparisons, not player-selected difficulty discounts.

## Alternatives rejected

One identical 40% threshold is simple, but it has no demonstrated fairness merely because the percentages match. It remains a useful control case for later testing.

A reference frozen before development makes the arithmetic superficially easier, but can leave an old accomplishment artificially large compared with the world that actually exists. Declaration-based baselines also reward early registration. Subtracting the claimant's contribution from the world changes the comparison to the rest of the economy and makes accounting harder to explain; it is not the same whole-world standard with a corrected error.

A weighted blend of incomplete opportunity and power progress can award a win without either full substantive achievement and can overvalue overlapping activity. Do not introduce such a score to make the bars meet at the same number.

These cases isolate extent. All nonnumeric requirements must be checked separately; passing this table is not a finding that the complete game achievement exists.

| Create 42 units, including 30 foreign | R=142. Opportunity 29.58%. Fail total extent despite sufficient foreign reach. |

Nine cases were evaluated arithmetically, including subset and overlap bounds. Additional checks confirmed that unrelated growth before qualification can reduce relative extent, customer independence without function loss need not reduce opportunity extent, and an additional qualifying unit increases the claimant's share in the stated growth example. No economy, AI or player simulation was performed.

Unrelated growth example: unchanged O=43 and Of=30 against R=163 falls to 26.38% and 18.40%. This describes a still-unresolved claim before a winning checkpoint; it does not retroactively revoke an already awarded victory, reset a fulfilled personal role, or replace accepted continuity protection for a fully earned package. Do not bank every partial claim against the smallest past world.

## Mixed strategies without a third finale

Recommend evaluating both full achievements automatically and awarding the applicable result when one complete test is met. A strategy can build connections, enable industry and retain substantial economic authority in combination. It need not complete two finales or choose a track at the beginning.

The same functioning activity may legitimately be evidence for both opportunity and power when it satisfies both definitions. Within either claim, it counts only once. In the 30-new/60-power example, the new 30 can be part of the controlled 60; it is not 90 units of commercial achievement.

The recommendation for this pass is not to add a separate mixed-score victory. Two incomplete expressions do not become complete just because their nonoverlapping union is large. This preserves the accepted Silk Road-style combination as a strategy while keeping the winning accomplishment inspectable. It is a proposed qualification resolution, not a claim that a new third mixed contract was already approved.

## Player experience

Lead with functioning places and consequences: the productive region now operating because a constraint was removed; the supply network whose allocation choices matter; the customer whose local capacity now stands on its own.

When relevant, explain the two results in plain language: “Productive capability you helped make possible” and “Economic activity your decisions currently shape.” Show actual known contribution and appropriately uncertain global reach. Exact numbers from this analyst fixture do not authorize exposing unseen world totals or private competitors' activity.

If a customer becomes self-sufficient, explain the consequence: its functioning achievement can remain part of the opportunity record, while the player's current authority may shrink. If an enterprise fails, explain the lost function. Do not make the player learn the distinction from a silently shrinking generic influence meter.

No recurring shipment tagging, per-city policy process, annual claim form, final vote, hold-for-N-turns requirement or compulsory project list follows. Cumulative ordinary trade and investment can still produce the complete accomplishment. Routine operation remains automatic.

## External comparisons and limits

The official base Civilization VI description uses different evidence for different victories: scientific space milestones versus cultural tourism. The transferable principle is that distinct accomplishments need not share a numerical progress system; it does not validate these commerce thresholds or recommend importing tourism complexity. [Official game description](https://civilization.2k.com/en-GB/civ-vi/). The relevant description was available in the indexed official result; direct page access returned an error, so no claims about additional page content are made.

BEA distinguishes gross output from value added and explains the removal of intermediate inputs. This supports avoiding repeated counting of production through multiple transactions. It does not supply Conquestoria's comparison coefficients, attribution rules or balance, and does not require a full national-accounts simulation. ## Evaluation and decision boundary

Gameplay: preserve two different accomplishments—creating consequential productive possibilities and exercising consequential economic power. Hybrid play can support either complete result. Actual cost and risk need later comparison.

Fun: useful capabilities and real economic decisions matter throughout the campaign. Neither a treasury total nor a commission paperwork loop is the achievement.

Excitement: a rival's independence, productive expansion or competing arrangement can change which complete result is within reach. Do not manufacture crises merely to keep a bar moving.

Playability: use one world reference and plain explanations of two kinds of evidence. Reject hidden denominator discounts and a blended score that obscures why the civilization wins.

Lovability: successful development can remain part of a civilization's story even when the customer becomes independent, while failed or inactive results do not receive fictional continuing significance.

Recommend accepting the distinct measures and unchanged foreign/world-reference principles, with 30/20 opportunity and 40/20 power as trial anchors only. Do not announce equal difficulty or final thresholds. The key comparison for later play is whether a compact creator and a compact organizer can each produce a substantial useful world change through consequential choices, at competitive costs and risks, without administrative work or a mechanically dominant route.
