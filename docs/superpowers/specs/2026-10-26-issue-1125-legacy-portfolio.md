# World-changing Legacy portfolio: advisory checkpoint

26 October 2026. Consolidated product-planning snapshot for [issue #1125](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/issues/1125), published at the user's request. This records accepted directions, candidate distinctions and explicit gaps. It is not an implementation plan or evidence of calibrated balance. Source and tests remain the authority on what the game currently implements.

## Purpose and design standard

Develop several satisfying ways to leave a world-changing legacy, supporting different personalities, independent leadership and genuine cooperation. Evaluate gameplay, fun, excitement, playability and lovability. The shared framework describes how an accomplishment qualifies; it must not force every candidate into the same research/build/deploy loop.

Legacy significance exceeds ordinary technological progression. The user's billion-lives comparison expresses transformative potential, not a literal population quota or permission to win from a tiny demonstration. Real application must be substantial and work across relevant conditions. A few tiles and repeated nominal demonstrations are insufficient.

Conquest remains the military victory. The proposed deterrence/peacekeeping alternative is parked. The candidates below are neither exclusive player classes nor the whole eventual catalog; expressive, spiritual and other cultural interests remain within the wider design. No arbitrary minimum era, final approval vote or ceremonial waiting period follows from this framework.

## Ocean: the applied-science reference

An exceptional national or shared effort establishes practical reproducible recovery and safe processing of consequential persistent marine plastic, demonstrated through real habitat recovery. Shore processing networks and mobile offshore approaches can provide different choices where physically supported. Actual recovery is useful before victory; routine operation and observation are automatic.

Qualification combines exceptional capability, world-relative recovered extent, regional distribution and meaningful environmental proof. The marine reference includes clean and polluted playable marine area. Supporting regions must each reach at least half the main region's qualifying extent: 8+4+4 meets that relationship; 8+5+3 does not initially. Protection for a fully earned regional package must not make beneficial expansion erase it, while genuine functional loss remains consequential. The 12% extent figure and earlier region counts remain trials.

Existing sufficient knowledge removes unnecessary research. No mandatory four-invention ladder or requirement to keep obsolete equipment when a recovered system genuinely sustains itself. See the earlier [applied-science consolidation](2026-10-21-issue-1125-applied-scientific-legacy.md) and [ocean research trial](2026-10-21-issue-1125-ocean-research-trial.md). These remain active advisory documents, not delivered gameplay.

## Five additional candidates and their distinct decisions

| Candidate | Intended world-changing result | Central player decisions | Main unresolved risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Planetary understanding | Substantial foundational understanding established through complementary evidence and consequential intellectual use | Which uncertainty to investigate, appropriate methods, access, synthesis and sharing | Repeating a known script, compulsory surveys, or an advisor choosing everything |
| Water engineering | Change where substantial civilization can thrive through viable physical provision | Source, geography, centralized/distributed systems, competing uses and relocation | Pipe-coverage grind, unsupported hydrology, burdensome controls |
| World commerce | Substantial newly enabled productive capability or exercised economic power | Where to invest, how to supply/organize, terms, counterpart alternatives and concentration | Opaque attribution, finance administration, nominal control and unavoidable foreign vetoes |
| Living cultural tradition | A developed tradition changes consequential public responsibilities and lived practice across independent societies | Authority, duties, displaced interests and independent adaptation | Generic capacity bonuses, passive influence and ideological bias |
| Agricultural transformation | Practical nutritious staple production with dramatically lower freshwater needs across substantial suitable conditions | Biological suitability, reproduction, adoption and use of released resources | Cosmetic duplication of Ocean or water engineering |

### Planetary understanding

The investigation-led experience is accepted. Complementary ocean and continental work addresses actual uncertainty. Institutions perform routine analysis; the player directs questions, methods and commitments. Knowledge can make further investigation more useful without guaranteeing a deposit, a precise geological event or political access. Satellites do not imply complete understanding.

Replayability comes from actual evidence, prior knowledge, equipment, geography and relationships. Do not randomize scientific truth merely to hide a correct answer. Sufficient existing evidence removes redundant expeditions; feasible actions remain available beyond recommendations. A foundational scientific achievement need not become a commercial product. Intellectual use abroad and the isolated-science boundary still need complete qualification definitions.

### Water engineering

The accepted candidate can use storage/distribution or distributed sources, treatment and reuse in appropriate combinations. Existing technology can support exceptional engineering. A famous dam, cheap power, one transformed town or many pipes is insufficient by itself.

Real sources, replenishment, treatment and simultaneous uses constrain the design. A reservoir does not create water; hydropower is not automatically consumptive. Relocation is possible but can have real costs, dissatisfaction and community consequences. Useful applications can serve existing mature settlements, without an obligatory long population-growth tail. Routine releases do not require per-city policies.

The 20%-of-world population, three applications, 5%-each and two operating-constraint figures are paper trials, not finalized requirements. Do not import Ocean's half-main-region ratio as a universal rule. Detailed operating and relocation definitions remain unfinished.

### Commerce

Accepted direction includes both creating opportunities and becoming a powerful force in the global economy, including combinations. Benevolence, monopoly, extortion and compulsory megaprojects are not prerequisites. Ordinary cumulative production, trade and investment can culminate in a substantial achievement. Current authority, historical creation and shared winner status are distinct.

Read the [commercial decision record](2026-10-26-issue-1125-commercial-legacy.md) for accepted measurements, availability, funding, attribution and the latest proposed campaign comparison.

### Living cultural tradition

The accepted example connects learning, actual public responsibilities and functioning practice. It is one cultural expression, not a requirement that all culture become public administration. Royal institutions, collective duties and independent local interpretations can qualify; no mandatory Western liberalization, individualism, common government or founder approval.

Foreign adaptation can become consequential and compete with its originator. History is not erased by disagreement. Routine teaching and appointments remain automatic. Solo cultural leadership involves foreign impact; a strictly isolated cultural finale remains parked. The material gap is a concrete useful institutional action and responsibility that changes ordinary play without a sprawling politics simulator or individual-official management.

### Agricultural transformation

Retain the accepted world-changing agricultural-science direction as an applied-science candidate. A useful crop must actually reproduce, work under the relevant growing conditions and become part of functioning food systems. A modest yield bonus or a technology name is insufficient. Do not disguise an equally prohibitive new input burden as universally accessible food.

Familiar-staple adaptation and a new crop/food system can offer different contextual choices. Actual released water, land or labor creates opportunities to choose among, not automatic rewards in every category. Avoid annual seed menus. Distinctness from Ocean is still conditional: biological adaptation and adoption must materially change what the player does before claiming another independent gameplay experience.

## Shared-world and ending rules retained

- Useful inputs between paths do not automatically grant shared victory or satisfy another candidate's whole achievement.
- Explicit shared undertakings require substantial fulfilled roles. Customers, allies, financiers and suppliers are not automatically winners. Qualified shared winners are equal despite differing roles.
- An activated substantial role promises a fixed result. World growth, ordinary cost changes or added partners cannot silently enlarge it. Real delivery, authorized funding and any explicit continuing duties still matter.
- Historical credit, actual current function and entitlement to win are separate. A failed enterprise is not a functioning legacy merely because somebody fulfilled an earlier delivery.
- Resolve all complete eligible claimants at the same normal checkpoint, including qualifying independent simultaneous achievements. No last-click or iteration-order priority. A later finish cannot join an already settled competitive ending.
- Survival is required. Settlers can preserve a route back; cityless units capable of obtaining a city or settler have the accepted provisional ten personal opportunities, speed-scaled. No resurrection of full military custody as an implementation requirement; city liberation was prioritized and full custody deferred.
- Capture transfers facilities to the conqueror. Foreign operation needs consent. Historical authorship does not transfer with ownership. Operation can require knowledge and capability.
- Ordinary knowledge spreads gradually, but extreme advances do not grant instant mastery. Theft and sabotage are possible; stolen knowledge/equipment may exceed the recipient's ability to apply it. Preserve known provenance and timing without per-discovery policy chores.

## Portfolio failure tests and stopping point

Removing the subject nouns must not reduce every candidate to funding research, building facilities, deploying repeatedly and waiting for coverage. A candidate needs useful decisions during success, not only compulsory crises. Large economies must not automatically dominate every form through money alone. Cooperation cannot create resources from friendship or force participants to wait for an unfulfilled role after the achievement legitimately finishes.

The portfolio does not yet demonstrate a credible independent peaceful finish for every compact hostile-start position. Do not hide that gap behind the distinction between solo and isolated, guarantee foreign acquiescence, or weaken world significance. Some setups legitimately constrain some paths. Information about unknown conditions must remain uncertain; a viability badge must not reveal hidden economies or actions.

What remains: candidate-specific observable functions and scope, causal attribution and economic representation, costs and timing, counterpart/AI incentives, shared information contracts, and playable experience evidence. Numerical paper checks and examples can reject contradictions; they cannot establish enjoyment or generated-world availability. No implemented victory or final balance is claimed by this snapshot.
