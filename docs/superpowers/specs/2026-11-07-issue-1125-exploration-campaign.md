# Exploration Legacy: campaign learning checkpoint

7 November 2026. Advisory backup for [issue #1125](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/issues/1125), read with the [portfolio](2026-10-26-issue-1125-legacy-portfolio.md). Records the paper campaign after the October checkpoint. Narrated outcomes, quoted prices and discoveries are constructed examples, not seeded simulations, implemented features or calibrated balance evidence.

## Player expression and standing instructions

The user's tested style is aggressive and independent: retain control, rarely admit others within borders, investigate promising unknowns, and recruit another crew when evidence and funding justify parallel work. Do not repeatedly defer a promising local lead merely because another crew is traveling abroad. Add a rig only when its use and funding justify it; another crew is not automatically another rig.

Crews look above and below land and water for discoveries and usable resources. Investigations can identify minerals, timber, spices, fauna, routes and other locally plausible opportunities. Monetization is a standing search objective, not a guarantee that every region contains a profitable find. Dead ends, unsuitable deposits, inaccessible sites and insufficient demand remain possible.

Seek recurring revenue where practical: extraction royalties, repeat surveys, service retainers, equipment/technology licensing, local-crew subcontract commissions, training, supply businesses and tourism. Lump sums can fund expansion too. Keep discovery and operating rights when negotiating access, inspection or observation work. A sale of information is not inherently a transfer of all underlying rights, but information already shared cannot be made unknown again.

The user accepted training trustworthy regional partners or partners not threatening the pursued victory, with contractual prohibitions on resale and independent manufacture. These terms express negotiated rights; they do not make leakage, theft, reverse engineering or breach impossible. Knowledge, equipment and the capability to apply them are different. Preserve provenance and discovery/theft timing when known.

Establish a remote base when sufficient evidence supports future exploration and monetization. Negotiate land, room to grow food and discovery rights. Use suitable crops with a saleable surplus where feasible. Permanent expeditions still need funding; treasury support is acceptable while seeking recurring income and retained royalties. Standing instructions need an authorized budget or reserve floor, not unlimited autonomous expenditure.

## Campaign examples worth retaining

- Private research investigated buried tributaries, submerged features, paleoshorelines and complementary continental evidence. The user regarded independently earned discoveries as theirs even where another civilization had related evidence; dual discovery remains possible.
- Buying a dry parcel, surveying it, then selling water or improved land illustrated blending exploration and commerce. The user bargained hard for ownership and resisted mandatory buyback options. This was a funding tangent, not a replacement of the exploration objective.
- Underwater and underground capability development ran alongside surface exploration. Additional crews preserved existing assignments when the evidence supported a third investigation.
- The user pursued recurring resource extraction royalties and retained rights to discoveries from commissioned dredging rather than treating all findings as one-time report sales.
- Exotic islands supported a tourism trial: raise rates by two after a profitable first season; after a profitable second season, investigate booking and excursion commissions. These were scenario-specific commercial instructions, not universal game tariffs.
- Reed Inlet work included a local pilot, exclusive new survey results, a wall investigation and a rig. A 46-meter sounding was a lead, not proof of a world-changing discovery. An offshore trough could dead-end while repeat regional survey work remained useful.
- Retainers must justify the crew commitment; otherwise subcontract to a local crew and retain a commission. The user's expedition should not become a permanently subsidized service desk.

Learning: a growing expedition network, discoveries the player feels they earned, and useful capability are stronger rewards than unexplained research points. Institutions handle routine analysis and crews continue authorized work. The player chooses commitments, risks, new directions and access terms.

## Rival and personality tests

The isolationist player tolerated a proven explorer near the border but would still refuse a base inside it. A useful track record alone did not feel like an imminent victory.

The cooperative/cunning host persona accepted a base conditional on receiving a survey copy, leaving only sale exclusivity to the explorer, requiring local supply purchases and luxury purchases on departure. Subsequent third-party sales made the explorer a threat. At future renewals the host sought higher terms, commission and veto rights on future sales. Such rights require agreement, apply within their negotiated scope, and do not retroactively erase a completed sale or grant control over the explorer's whole portfolio.

An isolated rival seeing its own grain ship gain a useful new route recognized both another civilization's progress and a strategic vulnerability to control. This was a stronger warning than a distant report about points. Rival awareness must follow observable results and legitimate intelligence; do not disclose hidden routes, customers, sources or private intentions to set the scene.

Both explorer and host could make substantial progress, but neither automatically earns the other's Legacy or shared victory. Funding, customers, hosting, current authority, discovery authorship and qualifying contribution remain distinct. Two regional successes were judged insufficiently far-reaching.

## Qualification and remaining work

Exploration must establish substantial understanding and consequential use across meaningful conditions. Mapping a few tiles, selling a report or accumulating customers is not sufficient. Findings can matter intellectually or through navigation, settlement, resource access and other actual capabilities. A single major discovery can have broad distributed consequences; do not require an arbitrary count of different inventions or ocean touches.

Private self-operated use can support an independent route where world significance is met; foreign adoption is not an automatic requirement for every scientific/exploration candidate. No complete quantitative exploration finish rule was established by this campaign. Do not import the later water-population trial as that rule.

The paper campaign exposed two limits: the narrator can conveniently supply a profitable opportunity whenever funding runs low, and omniscient scene-setting can conceal what players actually know. Future tests must include failed monetization, priced crews/equipment, rival alternatives, limited information and a reconciled campaign budget. They must assess discovery and exploration as a 4X experience, not require an economic-management game.

The [water campaign](2026-11-07-issue-1125-water-engineering.md) subsequently developed the shared operating principle: ordinary deals execute standing strategy; exceptional commitments surface when they change that strategy. This preserves the user's independent style without making every player negotiate every invoice.
