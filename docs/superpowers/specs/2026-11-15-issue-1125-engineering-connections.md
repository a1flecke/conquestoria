# Engineering connections: advisory checkpoint

15 November 2026. Product-planning backup for [issue #1125](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/issues/1125), read with the [portfolio](2026-10-26-issue-1125-legacy-portfolio.md), [water checkpoint](2026-11-07-issue-1125-water-engineering.md) and [fixed passage rehearsal](2026-11-15-issue-1125-engineering-passage-rehearsal.md).

This records the subsequent campaign, accepted direction, rejected measurements and latest proposed revision. It is not an implementation plan, calibrated victory specification or evidence of player enjoyment. All fictional prices, outcomes, geography and schedules are paper assumptions. Source and tests remain authoritative for implemented behavior.

## Current decision and stopping point

The user explicitly requires an undertaking with the demonstrated impact of Suez or Panama to qualify. Those are positive design tests, not named wonders that automatically award victory. Significant operating connections can qualify despite a concentrated construction footprint, existing alternative routes, uneven benefits between customers and a minority share of world trade. New industrial production is not compulsory.

Working definition: create and operate a connection that substantially reshapes movement between major parts of the world, with demonstrated benefits across those areas. This could be a canal, tunnel, restored waterway or integrated system; judge the effect, not the structure's category or appearance. Even an ordinary-looking port can qualify if it actually establishes an extraordinary connection.

The user accepted simplifying the design around a satisfying 4X experience. Shared requirements for functioning achievement, substantial scale, breadth and survival can use different observable evidence for different candidates. Do not build a universal historical-significance calculator or trace every delivery through the entire economy.

The latest rehearsal exposed weak choices rather than validating the experience. The locks/reservoir option wins sooner and costs less than the direct cut; future spare capacity and upkeep savings do not balance that near a competitive ending. A small price concession restores qualification too cheaply to be a dramatic decision. The fixture lacks established rival victory timelines and competing uses of national resources.

Next proposed revision: begin at the civilization-level commitment, with competing opportunities already established. Decide which major connection to pursue, acceptable ownership/access terms and what other priorities to postpone. Advisors can recommend clearly better engineering methods. Do not fabricate defects, customers or a sudden rival deadline to rescue the scenario.

## Experience and player constraints retained

- Evaluate gameplay, fun, excitement, playability, lovability, replayability and different personalities, including ages 7–43. Paper arithmetic does not establish enjoyment.
- Discoveries and works must create useful capabilities before victory. A failed attempt can retain a valuable enterprise without earning a consolation victory.
- The user's style favors sovereignty, control, recurring revenue, royalties, aggressive evidence-backed expansion and staged investments with observation. Property under another civilization's government does not meet the same preference as sovereign territory. Helen and Lizzie may accept other arrangements; these are user-provided style hypotheses, not completed playtests.
- Preparations before opening matter: fleets, customers, destination capacity, funding, sources and permissions can be developed in parallel. Do not repeat completed preparation after commissioning.
- Routine pricing, bookings, renewals within approved terms, maintenance and scheduling follow standing policy. Surface substantial investment, access, exclusivity, ownership, risk and strategic commitments. A solved area should not generate invented faults to remain busy.
- Independent operation is viable without compulsory shared ownership, victory or foreign operation. A shipping arrangement does not automatically grant inland access. All-domestic use can count if its actual scale and breadth qualify.
- No universal minimum era, final approval vote, compulsory disaster, extra ceremony or mandatory final hold timer. Actual commissioning/operation can culminate sufficient accumulated evidence. Resolve winners at the normal shared checkpoint.
- Survival and current function remain required. Historical authorship, current ownership/operation and eligibility to win are separate. Capture transfers facilities; foreign operation requires consent and can require knowledge/capability.
- Rivals respond through real interests, personality and information. Competition, cooperation, refusal and indifference are possible. No mandatory anti-player coalition or scripted assault whenever a project succeeds.

## Campaign correction: regional success was not a world victory

An earlier paper conclusion used a 20+10+10 industrial-impact result to announce a win. The user said the geography sounded like the southeastern USA. That verdict was withdrawn: the home, upland and coastal works could all be one regional undertaking. Preserve their practical success; do not retrospectively stretch the map or label invented numbers as proven world impact.

The subsequent schematic world remains fixed within that campaign:

- Western landmass: existing home/upland/coastal network at its southeast (A); northern basin (B) farther north on the same landmass.
- Eastern landmass: plateau prospect (C).
- Southern landmass: distant from the western home region.
- Island chain between the western and southern areas: operating seasonal base (D).

Construction footprint differs from impact footprint. Solid operating sites and distant visiting ships do not prove broad transformation. No comprehensive demographic totals, world production, voyage times or complete trade topology were established. Do not assign new shortcuts or favorable geography merely to validate existing spending. The separate November 15 rehearsal does not replace this map.

## Qualification: retained principles and retired shortcuts

Assess substantial change, substantial useful operation and substantial geographic consequences together. They must belong to the same undertaking; an unused spectacular shortcut cannot borrow traffic from unrelated services.

Useful evidence includes newly sustainable access, major capacity improvements, dependable service under relevant recurring conditions, and substantial reduction of complete journey requirements. None requires that the old route be physically impossible. Increased output is one form of engineering achievement, not its universal definition.

Measurement boundaries:

- Count real origin-to-destination service, not each intermediate stop. Separate useful return cargo can count; circulating the same goods cannot.
- One delivery does not credit a destination's whole population, industry or all subsequent economic effects. Establish useful demand without tracing every downstream transaction.
- Geography is distinct from political ownership. Splitting ports, borders or organizations cannot multiply breadth; conquest alone cannot erase genuine geographic reach.
- A tiny international market cannot win through 100% share. First knowledge of a resource is likewise insufficient. Minority global traffic can nevertheless be world-significant; majority world coverage is not a universal gate.
- Maintain the distribution of journey benefits. A spectacular saving for a tiny customer cannot certify all other traffic. Additional marginal service cannot dilute an already qualifying subset.
- Compare competent, usable alternatives under comparable technology and conditions. Deliberately wasteful routing, withdrawal or destruction cannot manufacture the baseline. Hypothetical permissions and undiscovered routes are not immediately usable alternatives.
- Include handling, return journeys, normal disruptions and supporting operation. Do not equate fee transfers with physical efficiency or convert unlike resources into a falsely precise net score.
- Freed fleet capacity can remain reserve. Reinvestment is not required to make a real saving count.
- Useful public/subsidized operation can qualify if sustainably funded; profit and toll income alone do not prove engineering transformation or commercial power.
- Supporting areas must matter. The user's 8+4+4 principle rejects token satellites, but its exact formula is not automatically a transport rule. Beneficial growth of the strongest area must not invalidate established supporting achievements.

Numerical experiments, not current requirements:

- The water-specific 50%-of-world-population trial remains scoped to that earlier population-focused candidate. It does not govern canals or all engineering.
- A 25% journey improvement, or roughly one-third less fleet commitment, was explored. One-third was explicitly removed as a mandatory gate after the Suez/Panama discussion. Unequal route improvements must remain possible.
- Earlier industrial-access tests used 40% newly enabled activity and balanced contribution subsets. For a three-region fixture, capping every contribution at twice the smallest gave 20+10+10 = 40, 30+10+10 = 40 and 40+15+5 = 25. These are not world-scale proof or a universal three-region requirement.
- A proposed Q >= (2/3)B comparison separated balanced qualifying activity Q from other activity B to avoid penalizing growth of the same undertaking. It is not 40% of current total world output, has not been validated and must not be generalized to connection efficiency. Devastation and eventual new-normal timing remain unresolved.
- The fixed passage rehearsal's 80 loads, eight-turn accounting window and three named connections are fixture values. They are not newly approved Legacy thresholds, an era floor or a mandatory eight-turn wait.

## Other 4X games: findings and limits

| Example | Documented approach or criticism | Design lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civilization V: Brave New World | Replaced five policy trees plus Utopia with tourism overcoming remaining civilizations' culture; trade, diplomacy and cultural actions affect pursuit | A peaceful path can engage the wider world through ordinary strategic systems. Do not copy the tourism arithmetic as proof of engineering significance |
| Age of Wonders 4, 2023 Watcher redesign | Developers described original magic victory as passive, defensive and easy to stumble into; redesigned around binding Ancient Wonders on the map | Make pursuit deliberate and geographically consequential. Do not import compulsory final countdowns or attacks |
| Humankind | Fame rewards varied activities; reviews differed between praise for flexibility and criticism of repetitive numerical milestones | Preserve different pursuits without turning breadth into another-port/another-customer repetition |
| Old World | Contextual ambitions offer an alternative victory; 2025 National Ambition changes expose the final objective earlier. | Endless Space 2 | Manual defines economy victory through cumulative Dust and wonder victory through specified monuments | Clear abstractions are useful, but wealth and construction alone do not meet this project's realized-Legacy requirements |

Developer explanations establish mechanics and diagnosed problems, not universal reception or proof a redesign succeeded. Reviews are attributed individual assessments. No family playtest has established that the Conquestoria candidate is fun.

## Simplified experience and next test

Accepted direction: small sets of explicit, observable accomplishment rules for each candidate within the shared framework. An advisor should explain what can change, the concrete requirements and known uncertainties before major investment; no discretionary historical verdict after spending. Respect fog and earned information when displaying forecasts or rival progress.

The finite rehearsal attempted three decisions: engineering method, establishing use, and a rival response. Its results are preserved separately. The three-decision limit was a paper-test constraint, not a gameplay rule. Preparation, useful operation and reserve preservation worked arithmetically; the dramatic-choice claim failed. The latest revision is proposed, not validated: move consequential choices toward the earlier national commitment, keep routine detail automatic and let successful operation provide a short payoff rather than adding filler after opening.

Before another playthrough, establish competing priorities, rival progress visible through legitimate information and any opportunity deadlines. Assess independent, cooperative and conquest-oriented choices without giving any of them guaranteed demand or a compulsory solution. A rival may respond during development; no new anti-player crisis should be invented after the fact. Show the actual passage, connected places and benefits at the finish. Existing useful service and historical contribution should remain legible on an unsuccessful attempt.

Remaining gaps: final observable thresholds, geographic definition, map opportunity frequency, early/late development calibration, operational scheduling, competing-victory pacing, information boundaries, cooperation and actual enjoyment. Neither a general formula nor an implementation-ready MR exists.
