# Legacy and the engineering passage

Product design for [#1125](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/issues/1125), 2026-11-16. This round proposes the contract for an opt-in prototype. Source and tests govern current behavior. See the [prototype evaluation plan](../plans/2026-11-16-issue-1125-engineering-passage-prototype.md) for cases, feasibility checks and the next decision.

## Product decisions carried forward

- A Legacy records a world-changing result that actually works. Discovery, construction and intermediate operation should improve normal play even when another civilization wins first. Use different observable requirements for different accomplishments. Applied science, foundational discovery, extraction, culture, commerce, and engineering can support each other without automatically sharing qualification.
- Several ambitions can coexist. Commitments compete for real money, capacity and access. Reprioritizing preserves prior achievements and obligations. Routine operation follows authorized terms. Warn before material changes to spending, rights, roles or risk. Funded expeditions keep their assigned people, equipment and funds. Prospective expansion can wait when uncommitted funds are scarce. Standing monetization instructions search for opportunities within authorization.
- An independent claimant may use ordinary partners, contractors and customers. A shared undertaking requires explicit agreement on fixed, substantial, complementary roles before the corresponding work is done. A qualifying role has equal standing at the finish. Removing someone from the active plan preserves their earned role and history. Survival, explicitly continuing duties and established qualifying breach still govern eligibility.
- Historical authorship, present ownership, operation and eligibility are distinct. Genuine loss of a required function can stop an unfinished claim. Modernization preserves applicable evidence if the same function continues and must demonstrate materially changed operation. Useful existing technology can remain in service. A rival earns credit for its own achievement. Rivalry and refusal of new work leave prior functioning agreements and historical credit intact.
- Settle the first qualifying competitive round at one world checkpoint. Collect all independent and shared Legacy qualifiers from the same settled state. Continue-play design must preserve the original result and record later accomplishments separately. Ordinary intel reveals real observable consequences at the scope earned by the viewer. AI pursues interests with its own legitimate information.
- The broader portfolio includes builders, merchants, diplomats, scholars, explorers, cultural and spiritual leaders, conquerors and protectors. A cultural claim needs consequential foreign practice for the current candidates, with room for independent local adaptations. Commercial opportunity and exercised economic power remain separate accomplishments with different proof. Engineering can include ecological recovery and ambitious physical change. The isolated cultural finale, exact cultural annexation rules, deterrence/peacekeeping as a military alternate, broader succession and breach rules, and most portfolio qualification and balance remain follow-up decisions.

The [November 15 checkpoint](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/issues/1125#issuecomment-6904749148) governs the sequence. The [pinned planning stack](https://github.com/a1flecke/conquestoria/tree/63820617b90636c193a5528b4f5f3d120350d558/docs/superpowers/specs) retains the detailed evidence and candidate decisions. The withdrawn regional victory, universal majority-share or one-third journey-saving gates, and treating the prior 80 loads, eight rounds and three trade links as general requirements are superseded here. Ocean and water population figures and the commercial 30/20 and 40/20 figures retain their own trial status.

## Lessons from other 4X games

| Evidence | Design consequence for the passage |
| --- | --- |
| Triumph said the original **Age of Wonders 4** Magic Victory was easy to stumble into, encouraged defending clustered improvements and required little exploration. Watcher moved the pursuit onto valuable map locations. [Developer diary](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/age-of-wonders-4/news/age-of-wonders-4-dev-diary-21) | Make the site and access decision matter. Record actual changes in the shared world, with counterplay arising from existing territorial and commercial interests. Once the result is earned, resolve it that round. |
| **Old World** exposed National Ambitions earlier and listed them in its Encyclopedia. The same update fixed a case where the AI could win when the human achieved a victory on the same turn. [Update #133](https://mohawkgames.com/2025/03/26/old-world-update-133/) | Explain the goal before an expensive commitment, offer a persistent place to rediscover it, and evaluate same-round candidates from one settled snapshot. |
| **Civilization VI: Gathering Storm** makes the Panama Canal a real change to naval traversal, with specific terrain and adjacency requirements. [Official manual](https://cdn.2kgames.com/manuals/civilization-vi/gathering_storm/2KGMKT_CIV6_Gathering_Storm_PC_Online_Manual_V7_US.pdf) | Make the geography and legal crossing readable before building. These precedents support design hypotheses. The playtest determines how the passage feels in Conquestoria.

## Engineering experience

**Promise.** Open a dependable connection between major parts of the world and see ships, trade and useful supplies follow it. A Suez- or Panama-scale change is a positive design case even with uneven savings and minority world volume. A named canal wonder, a busy local dock and speculative capacity are useful comparisons for insufficient evidence.

1. **Recognize.** Make “Engineering Legacy” explainable in the existing Victory Progress surface from the start of the opt-in scenario. It lists only known opportunities and stays reachable when there is no surveyed site. The first known site appears in an advisor notice with a direct link to its map detail. Show the known detour, affected ports and their uses, estimated round-trip improvement and known access requirements. An established alternative competes for the same uncommitted funds and has an observable benefit and timing. Explain visible rival pressure through ordinary intel.
2. **Commit and prepare.** The player chooses whether to fund the connection now, defer it or use the same capacity elsewhere. Present total authorized capital, reserve and operating implications, usable transport, essential permissions and a useful early outcome. Show any consequence to a planned expedition expansion before confirmation. An obviously superior alignment is an advisor recommendation. Use a known and affordable alternative only where geography or terms make it a real choice. Build and prepare ships or routes in parallel where legal. In the shared case, one party establishes and keeps the link available under agreed terms, while the other supplies a material part of the useful freight service. Specify the role's deliverable and continuing duty before consent.
3. **Operate.** Commissioning changes legal naval movement and route estimates. Make the first crossing visible without interrupting turn flow. The first settled import shows its named destination, what arrived and the useful national action it enabled. Ships and ordinary standing orders do the rest. The first slice uses consenting foreign suppliers and owner-operated ships for the independent case. A wholly domestic connection remains viable in product design when actual substantial use can be shown.
4. **Respond and resolve.** An already-visible rival development can compete for scarce capacity or substitute for some service. An actual closure or lost right can remove needed function. Show the ordinary consequence first, then the impact on the pursuit and the actions that are genuinely available. A prudent modernization can keep the same achievement alive. If the player ends with a profitable regional shortcut, show the operating benefit and the geographic requirement they missed. If the passage qualifies, show connected places, actual uses and credited parties at the common checkpoint.

## Prototype qualification

The authored scenario declares its geographic regions and representative port pairs before play. Macroregions reflect separate major coastal economic areas served by the connection. A city split, treaty, capture or renaming leaves the region tags intact. The prototype evaluates one connected undertaking even if the party structure changes. Apply all four predicates together to the same post-settlement state.

| Predicate | Proposed observable rule for the authored fixture |
| --- | --- |
| Substantial change | Compare the fastest legal complete trips with this passage available and disabled, with the same ports, hull profile, movement allowances, technologies and rights. The comparison includes both port service approaches and return movement. An enabled trip must traverse the passage and be at least two world rounds faster than the best disabled trip. Confirm that the trade runner can realize the qualifying trip under the same conditions. Ignore temporary unit congestion for this structural estimate. Leave newly possible connections for a separate fixture. |
| Useful reach | Two different unordered pairs of authored regions, together spanning at least three regions, each need two distinct qualifying loaded returns. Count a qualifying return when a carrier actually crosses the passage with authorized fixture stock and settles at least one unit at its intended home under the expected owner. The fixture names the consuming use and shows the first settlement satisfying an actual need. Anchor a voyage to one manifest issuance using stable issuance identity across reloads. Several claims or passage steps in one return give one proof. Several routes using the same region pair still give one corridor. Preserve buyer, supplier endpoints, operator and region IDs at the point of use and settlement so a later capture or renaming cannot rewrite the evidence. Canceled cargo homed elsewhere, captured or lost goods, pickup and a per-round income tick give zero freight evidence. |
| Current function | The passage is commissioned and open for the counted operators. Each corridor has a legal repeatable service with adequate authorized funding, usable carrier, continuing demand and a supplier able to meet it. Check this from the same settled state as qualification. A temporary movement delay leaves the service operating. A route suspension, exhausted funding, closed passage, lost right or lost carrier makes that service unavailable until restored. Qualifying returns must fall within the 12 most recently completed rounds inclusive for the authored fixture. Refusing new negotiations alone does not suspend a valid agreement. An authorized like-for-like replacement keeps recent proof on the same ports, service and operator without extending the window. A material change in passage alignment or service terms requires one new qualifying return per affected corridor before earlier applicable proof can count. A new operator earns its own role evidence. |
| Eligible claimant | Use reconciled civilization liveness. An independent claimant established the passage and supplies the qualifying current service on both corridors. An explicit shared agreement records fixed roles before their work. The commissioning party establishes and keeps the passage available under the agreed terms. The freight party operates a named corridor and supplies both qualifying returns there. The commissioning party supplies service on the other named corridor in this fixture. Once the collective result and individual duties are met, both earn equal standing. A routine seller, nominal participant or reimburser earns no shared berth. The fixture tests a removed member who previously earned the role and still fulfills continuing duties, and a member who never fulfilled it. Actual breaches and broader succession belong to a separate product decision. |

The two-round saving, two returns, three authored regions and 12-round window are prototype controls. Their pass demonstrates that the interaction and evaluator work on an authored geography. A production proposal needs a separate measure of world significance that distinguishes a formerly marginal geographic connection from a local detour, accounts for traffic actually served versus credible alternatives, and handles very uneven benefits. Test against Suez and Panama, an all-domestic case, and tiny international trades that must fail. Show new connections with no previous viable route in their own fixture. Keep production victory unavailable until these cases and cross-victory arbitration are settled.

## End-of-round behavior for this prototype

Evaluate once after movement, trade settlement and liveness reconciliation, using the completed round number even if existing finalization advances the world counter before presenting results. The prototype reports a dated first engineering result in its own opt-in experience. Equal qualifiers share the result. Sorting identifiers controls display order only. Later qualifiers appear as later accomplishments. Normal end-game state and events still follow current priority: an existing terminal outcome is retained; current finalization checks Domination then Science; hot-seat all-human elimination is handled after simulation when no terminal game result already exists. For a round where both engineering and an existing victory type qualify, capture both observations and the canonical outcome. The prototype ends naturally when that game does. It need not implement continued play.

A production decision is still required about same-round Legacy versus Domination or Science, especially in the presence of civilization elimination and pending independence. Recommended next policy for testing is preservation of existing Domination/Science ordering with an explicit design review of whether a simultaneous engineering accomplishment earns a competitive tie. Production needs a single arbitration contract and a result representation for equal winners; an appended terminal check would silently privilege existing outcomes. The shared rules about same-round Legacy claims and preserved history still apply.

## Information and operating boundaries

Use one detail panel from the advisor, the surveyed map marker and the Victory Progress entry. Its default view shows the effect in one sentence, the known before-and-after path, the current meaningful blocker or milestone, and at most one primary action. For example, “Ships can reach Southport two rounds sooner. Imports still need an approved supplier.” Show alternate commitment, capital, protected funds, expected benefit, key conditions and operating cap in one comparison before confirmation. Show named ports, agreements, stocks and evidence in expandable detail. Mark estimates as estimates and show unknown routes as incomplete. Use text and line style as well as color to distinguish projected, open and blocked.

The owner can see known costs, rights, deliveries and obligations. A partner can see accepted shared terms and authorized evidence. A rival sees public effects they observe or learn through earned intel. The rival gets no private estimates, ledger, expedition data or deductions from hidden progress. A hot-seat switch closes private detail, clears map previews and drops pending notices and actions scoped to the previous player. A direct link reprojects permissions when opened. Pressing an old Confirm after the quote, access, actor or budget changes spends nothing and shows the permitted new facts. Consolidate notices around discovery, first useful delivery and a material loss or recovery that changes a decision; subsequent routine shipments stay in the history.
